SUNDAY STREETS
An original, self-contained HTML5 traffic game inspired by the road-network loop and clear visual language of Mini Motorways. This is an independent adaptation with original code and artwork.

HOW TO PLAY
Drag from a colored house to a shop of the same color. Cars automatically find a route, serve a visit, and return home. Each home has two cars. Short routes and separate networks are more efficient than one overloaded street.

TOOLS
Road: drag to build. Each ordinary or bridge tile costs one road tile. Water tiles also cost one bridge.
Erase: drag to remove roads. Road tiles, bridges and traffic circles are refunded. Express routes attached to an erased road end are also refunded. Cars reroute at their next tile; blocked cars wait for a reconnection.
Express: tap two existing road ends or buildings to create a fast elevated shortcut. Cars travel almost three times faster along it.
Circle: tap a road junction to reduce intersection delay. Tap again to reclaim it.
Undo: restore the last road edit. You can pause and redraw your city.
Keyboard: R road, E erase, M express, O circle, Z undo, Space pause.

THE EIGHT-WEEK PLAN
Every 38 simulation seconds, choose new supplies and meet new neighbors. Survive eight weeks, then continue endlessly. A destination becomes crowded at eight waiting visits. Its warning ring fills over 22 seconds; clear the backlog before it fills. Relaxed mode retains all traffic and growth mechanics without losing your city. A lost city can also be continued peacefully.
Three city layouts, four destination colors with matching symbols, an optional night palette, double speed, best scores, and automatic local progress saves are included. Weekly supplies cannot be collected twice. The simulation pauses while the tab is hidden; road drawing slows time.

Extract this ZIP and open index.html, or serve the folder on any static HTML5 host. All assets are included in code. There are no third-party scripts, account requirements, payments, or network dependencies.
