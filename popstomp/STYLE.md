# A hand-inked soda revue

## Shared visual rules

The world is a tiny 1930s-style soda theatre, seen from the front; the barrel tilts enough to expose a top-down interaction surface. It uses original silhouettes. Avoid imitation cup-headed characters, borrowed costumes and reference text. The actor is a soda siphon with a crown-like bottle cap, curved spout, enamel monogram panel and white shoes. Fruit is illustrated in a consistent family of simplified recognizable shapes, with tiny pie-cut eyes. Reuse this language for later original acts.

At 420 × 600 logical units, main outlines are 2–3 px; internal fruit marks are 1–1.8 px. Actors have flat colour, warm highlights and simple shadow silhouettes. Paper uses deterministic faint fibres and broken strokes; background architecture is lower contrast, with thin engraved mouldings and soft tank steam. This is hand-inked vector/Canvas art, not pixel art or generated watercolor paintings.

Character head width is about 64 units, body 62, fruit 27–40, full tub 368. The barrel occupies the lower 43% of the scene. The bottle and its glass pipe remain on the right; backdrop mouldings and curtain ropes remain outside the main hand interaction zone. Leg arcs originate from the visible host rather than disconnected floating feet. The foot casts a shadow, accelerates toward impact, compresses through its pause, then lifts away; fruit pops resolve in a short radial wave. Gold bubble fruit has a distinct upper cap and shine marks, ice is a polygonal transparent shell. Colour is not the sole distinction.

## Motion and focus

At rest: slight body sway, glove movement, blinking, background steam, soda bubbles. On press: a dashed footprint. After hold threshold: a wider footprint. On release: a single defined shoe impact. Chain effects arrive as successive pops; thin ring waves show their radius. Main feedback stays in the barrel. Juice flights curve into the bottle; ordinary droplets fall with simple ballistic motion. The transient effect cap is 240. Gentle Motion removes the camera movement and lowers rendering density. Every animation freezes in pause.

## Interface

Cream cards, ink borders, offset shadows, red primary button, sage tool button. Serif headlines and tiny spaced sans-serif captions echo printed theatre tickets. The same cap/star badge appears in fruit, collection, tool reward and bottle labels. Use 44 px or larger touch targets, short English verbs and visible in-world progress rather than paragraphs of instructions. Do not add unrelated emojis to the actors or introduce photographic imagery.
