PIP SPARKS — A LITTLE LIGHT

An original, self-contained HTML5 electric puzzle inspired by ElecHead's contact-powered circuitry and detachable head. Original pixel artwork follows the user's preferred Pip Is You style, with quiet dark rooms, clear silhouettes, a cute cream-and-gold robot, copper tiles and sparse English UI. Official Steam screenshots were inspected for the reference's dark teal machinery, gold power and small robot readability. No commercial artwork, generated images, external assets or libraries are included.

PLAY
Open index.html directly, or keep every file together on a static HTTPS host. All artwork and synthesized audio work offline. There is no installation or build step.

Left / right: if facing the other way, turn first. Press again to walk.
Jump: a two-tile directional leap with a two-tile-high arc; walls can interrupt the arc. Jump in the direction you face.
Toss: throw your helmet up to three tiles ahead. A detached helmet keeps powering copper while the body walks headless. Touch the helmet to join automatically, or use JOIN while next to it. Copper receives power from the helmet directly next to it, above it or below it, then floods through orthogonally connected copper. Separate copper islands do not share electricity. Powered bridges are solid, powered doors are open, powered pink beams are dangerous to the body. Doors closing on the body or helmet cause a short circuit. A helmet falling out of the world also causes a short circuit.

Find all sparks before entering the HOME lamp. The first room's lamp also needs its connected copper lit. No time limit, lives, purchases or randomness in puzzle rules. A short circuit freezes movement until undo or restart.

Mobile: large action buttons, hold left / right to repeat, or one swipe per move. Keyboard: left / right or A / D move, Up / Space / W jump, X / Down / S toss, Z undo, R restart prompt. Reduced-motion preferences are supported. Rendering slows while idle and skips hidden tabs; optional gentle sound starts after a user gesture.

CONTENT
12 authored rooms across three workshops. Every room has a verified winning route. Four completions unlock the next workshop. Hints become more specific across three pages; an optional step trail restarts the room and prompts each action while the player makes all moves. Golden sparks require a finish without the step trail at or below the displayed route length. Sprout antenna, moon antenna and gold crown unlock after 4, 8 and 12 completed rooms. A daily circuit rotates through all twelve rooms.

Browser local storage saves the exact room, helmet position, copper state implicit in positions, remaining sparks, 100-action undo history, active trail, progression, best scores, gold rewards and selected charm. Browser storage can be cleared by the browser or hosting platform; this is local storage, not a cloud account.

SCOPE
This is a compact independent puzzle adaptation, not the full commercial game. Movement and electricity settle after each action; it uses deliberate discrete hops rather than ElecHead's continuous platforming. No ten-second helmet timer or commercial levels. All 12 winning routes, real browser keyboard wins, touch activation, helmet power, undo, local save recovery, trail restoration, short-circuit recovery and portrait / landscape layouts were checked. Physical devices and embedded platform browsers may differ.
