# NIGHTWARD — The quiet hours

Original English HTML5 game informed by the observed duty loop in Rezona's Night Shift at the Haunted Animal Hospital. Reference title, first shift task HUD, coffee inventory, patrol death and retry were observed. A completed reference duty/shift is not claimed. Its source and assets are not copied.

## The loop
Tap a marked task to walk there by an obstacle-aware path. Hold WORK to spray slime or repair a fixture; releasing pauses work without discarding it. Tap a sleeping patient to carry them, then tap Recovery. Pick up coffee and take it to reception. Patrol warnings invite a short detour into a room. Finish a shift, receive best-only stars, unlock the east wing, repairs, isolation ward and UV cleanup. Eight authored shifts culminate in a mixed-duty night. No paid items, odds, lives, accounts or ads.

## Art rules
Real-time cutaway 3D hospital, deep petrol-blue floor tiles, ivory ceramic, brass trim, coral hazard lights and restrained mint phosphor highlights. Consistent rounded manufactured edges, legible silhouettes and modeled seams. Rear walls carry trim, charts, windows, pipes and notices; front partitions stay low so targets remain readable. Every tool is visible in the porter's hand. Patients change from sleeping floor pose to carried pose to tucked-in recovery bed. Slime shrinks and breaks into bubbles, repaired pipes stop steaming, lamps illuminate after a breaker repair. Do not substitute a background illustration for functional space.

## Character and animation
Original compact night porter, navy scrubs, folded cap, large amber eyes, small mask and satchel. Directional walking, foot cadence, turn easing, work lean and carrying arms. A striped long-limbed corridor warden gives a visible warning before contact; it cannot enter the rooms. Gentle motion disables camera bob and impact shake. Status changes use silhouette and icons as well as color.

## Mobile and validation
One scene tap plus one large hold button, optional keyboard action/target controls. Minimum 44 CSS-pixel controls, portrait and landscape layouts, resize/blur/hidden pause, explicit resume, bounded particles/audio and DPR 1.5. Fixed-step model separated from graphics, deterministic routes, save validation and replay guards. Check all authored shifts through ordinary actions, obstruction/path invariants, interrupted carrying/work, all unlocks, touch cancellation, real rendered layouts and deployed ZIP. Physical handset performance is not assumed from browser emulation.
