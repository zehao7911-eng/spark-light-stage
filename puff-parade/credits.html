Puff Parade

An original toy-garden adaptation of open-source Reversi.

Code roots

Actual move generation, directional capture scans, capture-pair collection and scoring are adapted from Kamil Kurach’s reversi-web, MIT, copyright 2021. Original Board and Controller files are provided in upstream/. See the complete MIT license and source manifest.

The Board class was parameterized for 4×4 and 6×6 gardens, diagonal bounds were fixed, and logging was removed. Controller flip-pair handling is adapted in core.js. New pass/game-over rules, bounded AI search, lesson/campaign, medals, cosmetics, persistence and responsive controls are included as editable source.

Art and sound

All garden, carved tray, tiles, characters, outfits and effects are original Canvas code. Sounds are original synthesized Web Audio notes. No upstream artwork, audio, fonts, Three.js dependencies, analytics or network assets are included.
