# Pocket Guild

A mobile-friendly HTML5 inventory auto-battler with original vector artwork.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser, or serve this folder as a static site. No build step, CDN, external fonts, account, or server API is required.

- Tap market gear to buy and automatically place it. Drag offers into a specific free space to buy there.
- Drag your gear to rearrange the backpack. Tap an item for stats and highlighted neighbours.
- Use Rotate, or press R. Shapes must fit without overlapping occupied cells.
- Place support gear beside weapons, shields, and food to improve their performance.
- Drop matching items of the same tier onto one another to fuse them. Adjacent matching items also unlock Fuse.
- Place recipe ingredients next to each other, select an ingredient, and Forge. The forge book shows five recipes.
- Shop locks preserve offers through rerolls and between rounds. Expand the bag for more space.
- Battle automatically against generated local rivals. Win gold and choose loot; defeats cost a heart but also award rebuilding gold and loot.
- Every fourth round is a champion. Continue for as many rounds as your build can survive.

Two starting classes, 22 item types, three equipment tiers, five forge recipes, shape rotation, adjacency bonuses, stamina, armour, blocking, critical hits, poison, burn, healing, champion rounds, loot choices, and local save progression.

## Mobile

Responsive portrait layout, pointer-based touch controls, drag edge scrolling, reduced-motion support, safe-area footer, and sound unlocked by player interaction. Battles pause while the page is hidden. Compatibility was checked in Chromium with 390×844 and 360×640 viewports and dispatched browser touch events. Physical-device testing is still useful for individual browser behaviour.

## Files

`index.html`, `style.css`, `game.js`, `assets/`. Keep these together. Archive root contains `index.html` for HTML5 game platforms. Artwork and synth sound are generated by original local code; no copyrighted game assets are included. This is a standalone single-player game, not connected to the official Backpack Battles service.
