# Whispering Folks

An original compact hand-drawn HTML5 hidden-object game inspired by Hidden Folks. Three authored places: Whispering Camp, Sunday Market and Wobbly Harbour. 24 distinct picture targets, dozens of reactive props, illustrated trees/people/stalls/boats/buildings, interactive tent flaps, three-tap bushes and wells, crate lids, doors, bell and flag reveals. Original Canvas paths and synthesized comic effects only; no commercial art, code, recordings, image generation, fonts, CDN or account.

Drag to explore, pinch/wheel or +/- to zoom, tap props and revealed targets. Target portraits show a short clue. ? moves to a broad neighbourhood area; it does not collect the target. Find 4/5/6 targets to unlock the next place, or keep exploring for all eight and a postcard. No timer, failure penalty or paid hints. First discoveries earn one stamp; fully completing each scene adds four. Paper styles cost 14 and 22 stamps, so all 24 discoveries unlock both. Current scene, opened covers, selected clue, camera and collection save locally. Restarting/replaying preserves collections and never duplicates stamp rewards. After all three postcards, Another Day moves a few hiding spots in these same three scenes; these are small remixes, not unlimited new maps.

Keyboard Enter starts, arrows/WASD pan, +/- zoom, H hints, Escape pauses. Pause and hidden tabs stop time and sounds. Mobile portrait/landscape, 44px controls, DPR1.5 and 30FPS drawing. Physical handset testing unavailable; browser touch/viewports simulated. Standalone index.html works offline and ZIP root includes it. No build step.
