New art and feedback

The juice-bar background, whole and cut fruit sheet, and jar/bomb/splash sheet were generated with OpenAI's built-in ImageGen as one coordinated art kit. Per-sprite alpha bounds retain complete leaves and stems. The renderer, directional slicing, droplets, scoring presentation, new synthesized audio, nine-serving progression, touch input and saves were added for this adaptation.
