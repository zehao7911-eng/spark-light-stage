AZURE AIRPORT — original compact air-traffic management game

Tap an aircraft, then its matching runway: triangle/coral or circle/blue.
You can also drag from a plane to the runway. Preview shows the approach.
Hold sends a selected flight around to its holding circle and frees its runway.
Occupied runways cannot accept another clearance until taxi/turnaround finishes.
Watch crossing routes, fuel bars and gold priority flights. Close encounters
cost a heart and send both planes around; fuel exhaustion diverts a flight.
Three mistakes end a day. Retry keeps all coins and permanent equipment.
Each day has a landing quota; diverted flights are replaced, so a day can still
be finished after a mistake. Four island palettes, growing traffic, emergency
arrivals from day3 and endless shifts. Stars reward incident/diversion-free days.
Permanent three-level fuel reserve, radar-warning range and turnaround upgrades,
each visibly expands airport buildings. Book replays unlocked days; leaving an
active day needs a second confirmation tap. Automatic local save and Continue.
Keyboard: Tab select next flight,1/2 matching runway,Space hold,P pause.

English UI, touch/mouse, portrait/landscape; p5Canvas2D,cappedDPR1.5, noCDN/WebGL.
Open index.html directly or put this whole folder on a static host.
Original code/art/synthesized sound; no generated bitmap images or commercial
assets. Directional gameplay reference Mini Airways on Steam2289650: arranging
traffic and separation. This is a smaller original island browser adaptation,
not that game's maps, art, physics, multiplayer or complete feature set.
