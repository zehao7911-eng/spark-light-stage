# Wobble Wars

An original small 3D browser battle simulator inspired by Totally Accurate Battle Simulator. It is not an official game, and it does not include original game models or other assets. Three original procedurally modeled unit types and six campaign encounters are included. Units use spherical rigid-body collisions and animated limbs, not full articulated ragdoll physics.

Select Clubber, Archer or Bruiser; tap the blue side to deploy. Tap an existing blue unit to remove it and recover its cost. Press Battle to start. During a battle the Clear button becomes Pause. Use View to cycle camera angles and Routes to replay unlocked battles. Wins unlock the next battle and save progress locally. Sound is optional.

Mobile touch or mouse. The game requires a modern browser with WebGL. Browser phone-size layout and input were tested; actual device compatibility depends on browser and GPU. Open index.html, or serve the folder over HTTP. Three.js and cannon.js are bundled under their respective MIT licenses in vendor/.
