## Scope and limits

Canvas 2D uses three small cached annulus images, a cached scene background, capped 70-particle celebrations, at most 14 audio voices, default DPR 1 / 30fps target, optional DPR at most 1.5 / 45fps target, bounded fixed-step catch-up, and paused simulation/render/audio. Targets are budgets, not physical handset measurements. Phone-sized layouts and native interactions were tested on the development computer; no physical Android/iPhone FPS, thermal, acoustic-listening, platform grade or real App-host certification is claimed.

The campaign is finite: twelve lock configurations, three illustrated rooms and twelve distinct miniature motifs. It is a designed clutch puzzle with illustrative cabinet projection, not physically simulated metal gears or a 3D scene. Final refinements after the full campaign enlarged the keepsakes, improved carousel silhouettes/depth, added marquetry, synchronized earned host notes and removed unnecessary paused HUD work; controller rules were unchanged and source/extracted checks were repeated.
