# Reef & Rice

A focused HTML5 pixel adventure: dive for fish, bring your catch home, and serve it at a seaside diner. This playable slice explores the day/night loop associated with DAVE THE DIVER, using original characters, Canvas art, and synthesized audio.

## Play

Open `index.html` through any static web server. No build step, external scripts, or downloads are required.

- Mobile: drag the left pad to swim. Tap the harpoon to shoot; hold for a stronger shot. The aim marker leads a nearby fish. Dash uses a little oxygen.
- Desktop: WASD or arrows to swim, Space to shoot/charge, Shift to dash, R to return.
- Near a chest or oxygen vent, the harpoon button becomes the interaction button.
- Return with your catch to stock the pantry. If oxygen runs out, a rescue keeps part of your catch.
- Open the diner. Chop in the gold zone, then serve. Better timing earns more income.
- Upgrade the tank, bag, depth suit, and harpoon; discover eight fish across three depth zones. Unused fish carry into later nights.

Automatic local saves preserve dives and service. Overlays and hidden tabs pause simulation. Sound begins only after a gesture. Saving is optional: blocked storage does not prevent play.

## Scope and technology

This is a playable slice, not a full commercial game: one dive map, eight fish, three relics, six milestones, four upgrade tracks, and a diner serving up to eight guests per night. The dive/stock/service/upgrade loop can repeat.

The game uses Canvas 2D with pixel sprites, prepainted terrain, a capped render resolution, and a 30 fps mobile render target. No WebGL is required. All UI is English. Touch layouts were checked at 360 × 640, 390 × 844, and 844 × 390, with desktop checks at 1280 × 900. These browser tests do not cover every physical phone.

All graphics and sounds in this package are generated by the included code. No reference-game assets are included. Saves are stored locally under `reef-and-rice-slice-v1`; clearing browser storage resets progress.
