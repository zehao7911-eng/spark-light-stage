UNDERRUN · REDLINE

A browser adaptation built from the complete open-source game UNDERRUN by Dominic Szablewski / Phoboslab.

Original engine and art

The original WebGL engine, sprite atlas, physics, world construction and enemy code form the foundation of this adaptation. Readable modified source and relevant original source files are included.

MIT license and original copyright notice

Redline additions

Compact arenas, nine-wave campaign, endless continuation, assisted targeting, touch movement, offensive dash, automatic overdrive, upgrades, scoring, saved wave checkpoints, responsive interface, impact effects and new synthesized audio.

Original terminal story, CPU objectives, Sonant-X and original soundtrack are not included. No third-party music or generated image assets are bundled. This is an independent adaptation, not an official sequel.
