# A sun-faded armoured postcard

## Shared palette

Parchment #f2e4c9, deep botanical ink #273f3a, bronze #bb7d3e, terracotta #be654c. Player armour uses sage steel and ivory stripe details. Enemy machinery uses copper/rust with dark green mechanical cores. Explosive barrels are ochre with dark bands; gold projectiles and warm dust communicate successful fire. Background walls have lower contrast than the foreground silhouettes.

## Six views of one world

Sunwashed Quarter: coral/ivory façades, striped awnings and a clock tower. Harbour Road: sea-glass walls and a striped lighthouse. Copper Market: honey stone, parasols and a fountain. Rosewater Passage: dusty rose façades, lavender parasols and flowering street trees. Electric Old Town: blue/violet evening, warm strings of lights and a cinema marquee. The Golden Mile: warm brass, stepped masonry and a tall sculptural column. These share a modular architectural kit, rather than pretending to be six separately illustrated asset libraries.

## Shape language

Chamfered tank armour, compact turret and continuous dark track belts keep the player recognisable at phone size. The tank is fully inside the camera frame. Foes remain inside the wide central road; façades and landmarks stay outside the playable lane. No decorative building is presented as a mandatory target. Barrel bands, drone rotors, crawler tracks and the heavy unit's large health bar distinguish targets without depending only on colour.

## Action grammar

Shells originate at the actual gun muzzle. A shot produces recoil and a brief warm flash. A hit gives a small impact; destruction adds larger dust, bounded metal fragments, a low ring and floating scrap/score. Barrels propagate real area damage. Twin fire adds a second projectile, not just an icon. A barrage has five separated strikes. Repairs briefly compress the player and show a shield callout; no defeat overlay replaces the encounter. Reduced-motion preferences remove camera shake.

## Interface

Editorial serif headings and compact sans-serif controls share the cream, green and bronze material family. A single large AUTO FIRE action carries play; four fixed upgrade cards expose permanent progression. Paper-backed armour and score remain readable over both daylight and night scenes. The overlay scrolls on short screens. Projected target areas are larger than the mechanical details and never require pixel-perfect aiming.

For expansion, preserve the player/enemy colour roles, street width, target silhouettes, camera clearance, fixed upgrade slots and physical explosion positions. New districts should first prove a distinct landmark and lighting treatment at 320-pixel width before adding more decoration.
