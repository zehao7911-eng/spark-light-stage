# Scrap Knights

An original compact HTML5 physics construction game directionally inspired by [Besiege](https://store.steampowered.com/app/346010/Besiege/). The official gallery informed muted miniature countryside, timber-and-metal machines, separate stone masonry, pennants and tangible destruction. All illustrated geometry, parts, levels, characters, UI and synthesized sound are original code-authored work. No commercial assets, recordings or generated images are included. This is a side-view browser interpretation, not the commercial game's 3D builder, maps or complete content.

Build a machine on a five-by-three workshop grid. Select a part and tap a square to replace it. All parts must connect to the protected cabin; wheels require a block above. The budget is 22 brass. Timber adds structure; wheels provide physical traction; rams collide with masonry; cannons launch arcing explosive projectiles and recoil; springs hop while grounded and gently help right a flipped machine; balloons supply lift and gentle air steering. Mass, center of gravity, flexible joints, wheel hinges and impacts all matter. Stressed bolts can break. Three editable blueprints provide a Scout, Low Rider and Hopper starting point. There is no real currency, purchase or ads.

Hold left/right to drive, the brass star to fire, and up to use springs/balloons. Cannon angle cycles between 35, 55 and 75 degrees. Knock every marked bell off its stone tower, then reach the green flag. Two optional golden keepsakes can be collected near your machine. Cannon blasts trigger nearby explosive barrels and can move your own machine. Eight authored contracts introduce hills, suspended plank bridges, low arches and high gates. Use the workshop to change your build and try again; this resets the outing, retaining stars and the blueprint. Alternate machines and emergent blast-assisted solutions are welcome.

Three stars: finish, collect both keepsakes, and use the contract's lean-build budget or less. Best stars are retained and capped at 24. Slate and Copper cabin paint unlock at 6 and 12 stars. Replaying never duplicates a reward. These are eight authored outings with replayable construction, not unlimited unique levels. Physics outcomes can vary with contact order and the way you drive; no single blueprint is guaranteed to win unattended.

Keyboard: A/D or left/right arrows drive, Space fires, Shift / W / up arrow hops or lifts, Q changes cannon angle, Escape pauses. Native touch supports simultaneous drive, fire and lift. Mobile portrait, landscape and safe areas are supported. Physical handset verification is not claimed; browser native-touch emulation is used.

Blueprints, active body poses and velocities, bolt damage, projectile slots, inventory, tower state, paint and mute save locally. Reload opens Continue. Returning to the workshop or opening another contract resets the active physics scene after confirmation. Workshop changes save separately. Pause, hidden tabs and blur release input and silence audio. Restored body poses are exact, but rebuilt physics contact caches may make subsequent settling differ slightly. Engine state is simulated at 60 Hz, drawing at 30 fps, DPR capped at 1.5, with bounded projectile slots and effects.

Open the packaged index.html directly or host it on any static site. All scripts and styles are inlined in the packaged HTML. No external fonts, assets, network request, WebGL or build step is required. Matter.js 0.20.0 is included under MIT; see MATTER-LICENSE.txt.
