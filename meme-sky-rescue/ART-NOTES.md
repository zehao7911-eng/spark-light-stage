# Artwork

Generated on 2026-10-31 with the built-in image tool. A single art direction requested a premium illustrated 2D seaside city with navy outlines, turquoise, coral and warm yellow. Separate transparent assets depict a capybara, pigeon, frog, toast cat, rescue balloon and trampoline. Four edited alternate expression frames preserve the characters' poses for bounce feedback. The original PNGs remain in the adjacent art-sources directory; the game uses smaller WebP files.
