DOODLE DRIVE
An original compact HTML5 squishy-driving flipbook, directionally inspired by JellyCar Worlds (Steam 1740930). Official gallery screenshots d24d3afe and 19735aa8 were inspected for pastel graph paper, heavy ink outlines, tactile car proportions, visible wheels and a playful paintable body. All drawings, car, maps, code, interface and synthesized sounds are original. This is not the commercial game, its assets, editor or full campaign. No generated images, external fonts, libraries, CDN, WebGL or runtime download.

Hold LEFT / RIGHT or A / D / arrow keys to drive. Hold SQUISH / S / down for low tunnels. Hold FLOAT / W / up / Space for balloon lift. Two-finger driving and abilities work together. Balloon fuel refills on terrain. Yellow pads bounce you. R or the circular recall button returns to the latest checkpoint; stars stay collected. No lives or mandatory countdown. Pause or Escape takes a break.

Eight authored compact tracks with low tunnels, convex slopes, spring pads, gaps, three optional stars each, moving bridge platforms, checkpoints and five graph-paper palettes. Finish, all-stars and under-par with at most one recall award three badges per route (24 maximum, never duplicate farmed). Star wheel hubs unlock at six badges; spiral hubs at twelve. Windy Remix reuses the eight authored geometries with gusts, not infinite unique maps. Active physics, checkpoint, star pickups, time, fuel and best records save locally. Hidden tabs/blur pause and silence audio. A PNG postcard can be downloaded.

The paint garage has six ink colours, a 20 by 10 body-paint grid, eraser, 25-stroke undo, clear and wheel-hub selection. Draw directly with touch or mouse; the body painting carries into gameplay and survives reload. Painted pixels clip to the genuinely deformed car chassis. This is a compact body-livery tool, not a full vector or track editor.

Physics: ten Verlet nodes (eight body, two suspended wheel centres), 45 elastic distance constraints, two substeps and six solver iterations at 60 Hz. All nodes collide against convex terrain; chassis nodes really move and compress. Wheels use circle collision with contact-based squash animation, rather than a full tyre-volume simulation. The squeeze input contracts the rest lengths and collision radii. A finite balloon applies upward force, not teleportation. Spring pads impart velocity. Airborne righting assist damps excessive chassis roll. Maximum per-substep displacement limits tunnelling. Original Canvas2D procedural pencil art, paper grain and hatch textures. DPR capped 1.5, 150 visual particles, 16 sound voices; no physical handset performance claim.

English interface, portrait/landscape safe-area layouts and at least 46px controls. Browser touch emulation is used for phone QA. Blocked local storage permits session play without persistence. Audio starts after interaction; mute persists. Packaged index.html is self-contained and runs offline or on any static host. Editable source files are included.
