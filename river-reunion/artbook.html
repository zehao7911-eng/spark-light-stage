One little world. Three afternoons.

Block-built campers use the same head-to-body ratio, square facial marks, articulated limbs and bright life vests. Warm timber, woven straps and light canvas distinguish equipment from cool water and faceted stone. Foliage uses grouped crowns with a lighter upper lobe; detail stays subordinate to the silhouette.

The river is the clear centre lane. Yellow floats and golden tokens identify friendly routes; neutral rocks read as solid obstacles. Long terrain layers and low-contrast distance fog frame the scene. Camera changes stay above the river, with a short pickup accent and a separate dockside arrival view.

Deep teal panels, warm paper type and paddle symbols keep the outdoor UI cohesive. The scene remains visible beneath a compact header and large bottom touch controls. Foam and splash are small, temporary and bounded, leaving characters readable.
