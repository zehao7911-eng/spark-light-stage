# Goo Valley
An original HTML5 elastic construction puzzle inspired by World of Goo's live-blob structures, silhouettes and pipe collection. Art, interface, physics, animation and audio are code-native originals. No external assets or libraries.

Drag a goo from the bottom supply into the scene, or drag from empty scene space. A valid placement links to at least two nearby balls. The preview shows connections. Triangles are stronger; move around rocks. Keep at least three spare goo for the exit. When your structure reaches the pipe, all spare goo crawl along its links and are collected. Fewer construction balls means a better star score. The top ribbon shows the three-star saving target. Lights near your construction grant an extra goo.

12 handcrafted levels across Meadow, Amber, Moonwater and Twilight, followed by repeating variations with mirrored layouts, evolving wind and new background terrain. Endless levels reuse the twelve puzzle layouts in variants rather than promising infinite unique puzzles. Stars and collected totals save locally. Each current structure and its undo history also saves; reloading resumes your current level. Replays keep the best star result. Finish the main journey to open endless play.

Verlet integration at 90 Hz, iterative distance constraints, fixed roots, rock collisions, bounded particle effects, expressive blinking eyes, synthesis audio after user interaction and optional supported-device vibration. Rendering targets 30 FPS with capped pixel ratio. No loss timer. Undo refunds the last placement, including reversing its light reward. Hint shows an attainable route point; it does not place a ball. Pause, restart, level atlas and mute. Backgrounding pauses.

English UI; touch and mouse; responsive portrait and landscape. Keyboard: H hint, Z undo, arrows move a preview, Space place, Escape pause. Open the standalone index.html directly to play offline. The ZIP includes editable art.js, game.js, style.css and icon.svg.
