BURROW BLOOM
Original HTML5 mining game.
Move with arrows, WASD, on-screen directions or tap beside the miner. Move into a block to dig. Matching gem veins burst together. Space / BLAST detonates a charge. E / CASH IN banks treasure and refills charges. Buy upgrades at the workshop. Each depth has a gem goal; cash in after meeting it to enter a new generated mine. Eight relics can be collected. Best equipment, mine and cargo save locally.
Serve index.html over HTTP. No external assets or network dependencies. Portrait and landscape touch support. Original code artwork and synthesized sounds. Inspired by mining and upgrade loops in SteamWorld Dig; no commercial assets included.
