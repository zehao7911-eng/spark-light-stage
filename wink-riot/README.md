# WINK RIOT · A Very Good Pop

A small, complete original HTML5 comic pop toy. Tap or swipe through expressive paper eyes, inflate striped eyes, and pop golden eyes for actual spatial chain reactions. No timer, lives, account, purchase, advertising or network runtime dependencies. All game text is English.

## Play

Tap/swipe ordinary eyes. Hold striped pink eyes for about 0.4 seconds, or tap them three times. Gold eyes pop every remaining eye within their actual chain radius, including chained gold eyes. Clearing the portrait earns ink tokens and opens the next portrait. The large POP AN EYE button provides a simple sequential alternative, including inflation for striped eyes. Keyboard Space does the same; S stamps, C uses the choir, Escape pauses.

Two completed portraits unlock Ink Stamp: eight pops charge a large area pop, which can itself begin new gold chains. Five completed portraits unlock Eye Choir: one free alternate-eye chorus per print. Reach upgrades increase direct touch and stamp radius; Echo upgrades increase gold chain reach. Each has three capped paid-with-earned-tokens levels. Nine finite authored identities and seeded eye arrangements form the campaign, using four original head silhouettes and nine coordinated palettes across three print layouts. Clearing all nine opens repeatable seeded Encores (maximum stage index 999, 24 eyes). Encores reuse the visual kit, not infinitely unique paintings.

One star for completion; another for a six-pop chain; another for a twelve-pop chain or using an unlocked tool. A chain is maintained by popping within 2.8 seconds. Replays improve best stars and chain; each deliberate completed replay earns tokens again. There is no forced grinding gate to the next portrait. Save key wink-riot.v1 retains exact popped-eye and inflation state, coins, upgrades, print records and settings. An active saved print resumes behind a Continue prompt. Fresh Print freely restarts the current print. A two-tap reset is available in How to Play.

## Art and sound

All Canvas artwork, silhouettes, gloved hands, paper collars, crowns, TV aerials, screen-print panels, hatching, pointillist ornament, expressive eye/lid/iris detail, confetti, ink rings, UI and synthesized sound are original code artwork. The visual kit uses dark aubergine outlines, warm paper, selective teal/pink/amber, controlled hatch lines and flat paper cutout shadows. It is intentionally a 2D comic toy, not pixel art or smooth 3D. No image generation, commercial art, logos, characters, recordings or external fonts are included.

The supplied art atlas's sketch-cutout gallery was actually opened and its enlarged Don't Starve Together screenshot inspected for paper silhouette, outlined material and selective detail. https://mygamesartstyle.jupiterthewarlock.com/styles/sketch-cutout/ . Its character, layout and art were not copied. The final comic-pop palette and original figures are this project's direction, not a claim of visual parity with that commercial game.

Reference: Rezona Eye Bloom, https://rezona.ai/game/pgcserver/play/QsDURwfE . Its title, Tap to Pop intro, illustrated head and emerging stalk eye were inspected and native clicks attempted. The reference score remained 0; no successful reference pop or full round is claimed. The direct-touch illustrated-eye toy concept informed this independent game. No reference code, artwork or sound is included. The initially auditioned Rip net-tearing toy was excluded after checking that Snipwing and Cut / Launch already use it.

## Run and modify

Open index.html directly or serve the extracted folder over HTTP(S). index.html and WINK-RIOT-OFFLINE.html contain all JavaScript/CSS inline. Only the optional favicon is a separate local file. There are no CDN/module/WASM requirements. Editable sources: index-source.html, style.css, model.js, art.js, game.js. Run `node test-model.cjs`; rebuild with Python `build.py`. The ZIP has index.html at its root, source, build helper, notices and SHA-256 manifest.

Mobile-first touch capture/cancel and short-landscape layout. DPR capped at 1.75 (1 in Gentle FX), drawing capped at 40 Hz (30 gentle), fixed-step model, 180 particle cap, bounded audio voices. Pause/background stop simulation and audio; saved state can resume later. Browser viewport emulation is distinct from a physical phone test; no device/FPS or platform grade is promised.
