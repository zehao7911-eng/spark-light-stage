# WINK RIOT visual kit

Dark outline #191b31, warm paper #fff1ce, watermelon #ff708b, seafoam #8ee2c4, amber #ffc45a. Nine palette tuples in art.js use this same contrast grammar. No imported bitmap assets.

Four reusable head silhouettes (sun cutout, television, paper coronet, cloud) with outlined eye sockets, inky mouths, eight broad teeth, paper collars, ties, cuff seams, expressive elastic gloves and registered offset shadows. Eye size follows the board density. All eye whites, highlights, tooth shapes and control areas share the warm paper colour. Interactable gold/striped types have shape/marking differences in addition to colour.

Three print layouts: angled broad panels and eye ornaments; restrained dark concert print with linear hatching; radiating carnival beams with deliberate repeated dots. Fine lines sit within a large readable silhouette. Nearby interactive eyes have the highest contrast and detail. No random noise overlays or inconsistent high-resolution illustrations.

UI uses compact bold sans for actions, an italic serif for portrait names and punchy collection headlines. The warm paper modal, ink borders, slightly offset pink shadow and original text symbols use the same materials as the portrait. Earnt tool states and selected actions stay obvious.

Animated pupil tracking, eyelid breathing, held-eye inflation, short pop compression, elastic head sway, floating printed words, paper confetti and gold/ink shock rings. No gore or realistic injury. Gentle FX reduces movement/particle amount and DPR, and keeps the actual game rules unchanged. Reduced-motion user preference is respected on first visit.

This is a code-drawn expressive comic/cutout direction inspired by the user's atlas. It does not claim to replicate a commercial art production pipeline. Extend using these silhouettes, ink weights and print patterns, rather than mixing in 3D or pixel characters.
