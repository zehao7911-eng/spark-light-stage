MOSSLIGHT — LITTLE PLACES, LOVELY DAYS
An original interactive 3D art prototype inspired by the cozy diorama building direction of Tiny Glade. This is a focused building sandbox, not a reproduction of its full simulation or commercial assets.
Choose Cottage, Tower, Wall, Path or Tree and tap inside the meadow. Drag to orbit in every tool; pinch or scroll to zoom. In Explore, tap a building to select it, then rotate, resize, recolor or remove it. Undo restores up to 25 recent edits during this session.
Tap wandering sheep. Switch between daylight, golden hour and overcast light. Save a PNG picture. The village saves locally after edits. English UI, portrait and landscape touch support. Maximum 65 user structures.
All models and procedural materials are original, generated locally. No remote dependencies. Three.js included under its MIT license in vendor/LICENSE.txt. WebGL is required. Open index.html or serve this folder as a static site. No build step.
