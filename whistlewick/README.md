# Whistlewick

A hand-built HTML5 incremental parcel railway. Original Canvas artwork, code and synthesized sound. No external assets, fonts, libraries, tracking or purchases.

Hold the railway to stoke the main train. Hold a built workshop to prepare extra cargo. Trains automatically collect cargo and unload at the post office. Deliveries earn coins; special deliveries earn blueprints. Build all three workshops and reach the delivery goal to open the next line.

Eight upgrade paths change the engine, carriage length, cargo production, fleet, delivery value, Express rush and clocktower. Deliver twelve local treasures across six illustrated landscapes. Later years preserve the fleet, equipment and passport, while increasing cargo value, workshop speed and goals.

Serve this folder over HTTP or upload it to GitHub Pages. Entry point: index.html. Mouse and touch, portrait and landscape. Auto-save and background/offline earnings (up to eight hours) are stored locally. Sound activates after a gesture. Settings include mute, reduced effects and a confirmed reset.

The miniature transport atmosphere draws inspiration from Train Valley 2; this is an original relaxing incremental game with no traffic failure or deadline systems.
