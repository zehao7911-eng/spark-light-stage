Keyholder is a single-player HTML5 adaptation of the residential property system in JonahPWR/Open-Source-Roblox-Games, Robbing Simulator.rbxl. Source: https://github.com/JonahPWR/Open-Source-Roblox-Games
house.js contains the extracted BuyableHouse instance 70471, its component hierarchy, size, colors, transformations and part classes. PropertyManager.original.lua is the original server script for comparison. The port implements buying, half-price resale, locks, lighting and curtains in JavaScript. Visitor requests, viewing fees, landscaping and interior furniture are new. External Roblox audio, uploaded meshes, multiplayer and the wider city are not included.
