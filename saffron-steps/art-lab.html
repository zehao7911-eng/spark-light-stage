ORIGINAL PIXEL WORLD / ART BIBLE 02

SAFFRON STEPS

Hand-laid roofs, planted windows and a little everyday magic.

Art board ↓

480 × 320 native pixels. Scroll the scene on a small screen; every pixel keeps its shape.

01 / One world, one grid

32 × 16 ground diamonds. Eight-pixel height steps. Warm top planes, ochre front planes and plum side planes. Every floor edge, counter, roof and stair uses the same 2:1 slope. Iri, Mallow and Pin stand on fixed foot pivots; buildings and props sort by their ground footprint.

02 / Cast & silhouettes

Iri: broad cap and plum apron. Mallow: square hat and carried planter. Pin: small brass crest and long tail.

03 / Living objects

Recognizable handles, rims and working surfaces take priority over texture. Bright brass marks objects worth touching.

04 / Material planes

Three main values per material. Distant architecture loses contrast and small marks; foreground detail stays in deliberate clusters.

05 / Interface & feedback

Prepare 140 ms → lift 100 ms → contact 80 ms → settle 180 ms. A tiny contact spark sits above the hand, never across a neighboring ground tile.

06 / Craft & light study

Gabled tiled roofs, inset joinery, planted windowsills, stepped plaster repairs, delicate cloth edges and a suspended dye line. Small objects have been resized for believable street proportions.

07 / Expand the quarter

Add a courtyard, a dye terrace and a night tea garden using the same height steps, roof rhythm, face lighting and foot anchors. Keep a continuous two-tile walking lane. Design new rooms from footprints before drawing their facade.
