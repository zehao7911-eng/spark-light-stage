# Saffron Steps — A Little Tea Quarter

An original, English-language HTML5 tea-garden game using the approved Saffron Steps isometric pixel-art direction. Canvas 2D, no engine download, no CDN, no account, no payment, no advertisements.

## Play

1. Select one of three guest orders. Leaf icons show the ingredients.
2. Tap a glowing garden in the scene, or **Pick herbs**. Iri walks to the right garden.
3. Tap the kettle, or **Brew**. Optionally tap Brew again as the moving dot enters gold for a perfect infusion (+4 coins).
4. Tap the guests, or **Serve**. The matching cup is delivered, rewarding coins, a visible burst and a short synthesized chime.

Guests have no patience timer. There is no failure or purchase pressure. A warm-welcome chain rewards fairly quick deliveries, but missing it never removes anything. Choose another order at any time. A new destination replaces an unfinished walking command. Garden baskets hold five of each ingredient. Herbs regrow; no currency is needed to grow them.

Keyboard: 1–3 select order, H pick, B brew/stir, S serve, Escape pause. All actions also have large HTML buttons. Scene taps use large hit regions. Sound begins only after interaction and can be muted. The page pauses on blur/background; Resume continues it.

## Progression

- Start: mint garden and mint tea, three guests, one cup.
- 4 delivered teas: saffron garden, saffron tea and sunrise blend.
- 8: berry garden, berry tea, garden blend and quarter special (six recipes total).
- 12: visible honey bees; every 12 active seconds they supply honey (cap three). Honey adds +3 to a delivery.
- 18: alternating lantern evenings and continued market days.
- Every six deliveries: a new day and +10 coins. Numeric days continue indefinitely; these are repeatable market days, not infinitely unique authored levels.
- Buy deep planters at 2 deliveries / 18 coins, copper kettle at 4 / 30, tea terrace at 7 / 42 and garden friend at 10 / 55. The terrace adds a second cup slot and furnishings. The friend gathers a missing ingredient for the selected order every six active seconds. Upgrades have one level each.
- Tea journal records the six distinct recipes served, perfect infusions and best welcome chain.

## Saves and accessibility

Local storage key: `saffron.steps.v1`. Saves include coins, upgrades, deliveries, journal, day, ingredients, regrowth timers, tray contents, active brew and orders. Walking commands restart from the central lane on reload, without duplicating rewards. Time does not advance while paused or closed. If browser storage is unavailable, the game remains playable and shows a save notice. Saves are per browser/device and are not cloud saves. Browser-reported mobile viewports and touch-friendly controls are tested; physical device performance cannot be guaranteed without a device test.

Photo saves only the scene as a PNG to the player's device. No image is uploaded. The game needs no network once its local resources have loaded. Serve this folder with any static web host; for local use, `python -m http.server 8000` and visit http://localhost:8000. Open `art-lab.html` for the original art kit and visual rules.

## Original art and scope

World 480 × 280 visible pixels, 32 × 16 projected ground diamonds, 24-color palette, nearest-neighbor rendering. Gabled roofs, planted windows, plaster, cloth, foliage and cast retain the original scene's pixel scale. Four ambient background frames, animated cast sprites, original canvas effects and synthesized audio. No commercial game images, characters, music or code are included. References are documented in ART-DIRECTION.md. Art was drawn through editable integer-pixel Python source, not generated by an image model.

Files: engine.js (economy, progression, save validation); game.js (navigation, input, rendering and sound); game.css; index.html; quarter-0..3.png; cast.png; props.png. Other images, build_art.py, font.py, palette.json and atlas.json form the editable art kit. Pillow is needed only to regenerate art. Runtime JavaScript has no dependencies.

The isometric scene is a compact fixed quarter, not a free-roaming city or full shop-building simulation. The visual study remains available separately. Future expansion can add recipes and neighborhoods without changing the projection.
