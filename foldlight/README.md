# Foldlight

Original HTML5 perspective puzzle adventure. Directional inspiration: FEZ by Polytron Corporation, https://store.steampowered.com/app/224760/FEZ/ . Four cardinal projections change which stones are adjacent, with smooth 3D-to-2D rotation transitions. All code, level layouts, character artwork, terrain details and synthesized sounds are original; no commercial assets or generated images are included.

## Play
Tap a glowing stone to hop there. Turn the world left/right to discover different connections. Collect three golden lights, then enter the awakened doorway. An optional hidden moth earns an extra star and follows the courier. No timer, falling, lives or purchases. Background horizontal swipes also turn the world. Keyboard: Q/E turn; Left/Right hop to a reachable stone in that direction; Z undo; H reveal a hint. Named stone buttons support Tab/Enter too.

Stones become neighbors if their projected horizontal coordinates differ by exactly one and their heights differ by at most one. Depth is ignored for this rule. If two stones overlap at the same projected position and height, the front stone is the landing destination. Turning does not move the courier or collect objects. Reachable stones and dashed links make this unusual rule visible. This is a discrete exploration puzzle, not FEZ's complete continuous platforming, commercial levels, open world or original assets.

12 authored islands across waking gardens, copper orchards and moon archives. Best ratings, collected moths, active logical state and up to 100 undo actions are saved locally. One star for completion; one for the moth; one for a no-hint finish within the island's optimal all-collectibles route plus four actions. Undo counts as an action. Hints highlight one next action rather than performing it. The journal replays unlocked islands. 12 total stars unlock a lilac scarf; 24 unlock a golden lantern/scarf. Finite campaign, not advertised as endless or procedural.

## Run / package
Open index.html directly or serve this folder on static HTTP(S). Six files, no build tools or network dependencies. The ZIP contains index.html at its root. English UI throughout.

Canvas 2D draws original voxel faces with masonry, moss, inscriptions, roots, flowers, waterfalls and distant ruins. Maximum 16 stones, 120 particles, 14 synth voices; rendering capped at 40 FPS / DPR 1.5. Smooth cardinal turns, courier hop/scarf/lantern animation, collectible bursts, follow-moth, optional synthesized audio unlocked on input. Responsive world fitting and at least 44 CSS-pixel active stone targets. Hidden tabs stop simulation/animation clock and suspend audio; reduced-motion preferences shorten hops and rotations and freeze ambient animation. Corrupt saves are rejected, denied storage falls back to in-memory play. Reload settles any cosmetic transition at the already-saved logical destination.

## Validation
All 12 islands were completed through actual browser controls: the first eight in a 390x844 portrait viewport, the final four using the landscape numbered-stone controls at 844x390. 36 stars / 12 moths and both cosmetic unlocks were checked. Native mouse swiping, Q/E input, undo, mute, a hint before the first move and exact logical save/reload (including best ratings and equipped skin) were exercised. Engine tests cover every optimal full-collection win plus 72 mixed action/recovery runs and corrupt-state rejection. Six viewport sizes (320x667,390x844,430x932,667x375,844x390,1280x720) have no page overflow and all visible gameplay buttons remain in view and at least 44 CSS pixels. Landscape includes numbered duplicate hop buttons to avoid precise selection on smaller stones. Browser logs were clear. These are browser viewport/mouse tests; no physical-phone or native-touch-event certification is claimed.
