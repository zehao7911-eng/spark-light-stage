PETAL PUTT — original pocket garden card puzzle

Choose a ROLL or JUMP card, then a direction. The number is the distance.
The four arrows correspond to the four edges of the isometric garden.
ROLL stops before a wall, empty edge or water, and immediately upon sand.
JUMP ignores intermediate tiles, but needs a safe landing. Paired flowers
teleport the ball after it lands. Reach the flag with the cards available.
Dotted lines preview possible destinations. Tap a destination or swipe across
the scene; directions are also available as large buttons.
Undo is unlimited. Restart during a played garden needs a second tap.
A hint suggests a solvable card/direction from the current state; if no route
remains, undo. Hint use remains recorded through undo/restart of that attempt.
Stars: one for completion, one without hints, one within the shortest par.
First clear awards 6 + stars petals; a replay awards 2 + stars. Spend petals
on four ball styles; unlocked styles, stars and progress are stored locally.
24 distinct authored/solver-checked puzzles across four garden palettes;
further rounds rotate these boards. This is remix play, not unlimited unique maps.
Album replays unlocked gardens; abandoning progress requires confirmation.
Auto-save and Continue, paused animation, mute and optional original sounds.
Keyboard:1..7 cards,arrows/WASD directions,Z undo,R restart,H hint,P pause.
English UI; portrait/landscape touch and mouse. p5 Canvas2D,DPR capped1.5,
no CDN,WebGL,generated bitmap or commercial art. Open index.html directly
or upload this whole folder to a static host. Private storage may prevent saves.
Original code and garden drawings. Gameplay inspiration: Golf Peaks, Afterburn,
Steam923260. This is an original compact browser interpretation with its own
maps and visuals; it does not contain that game's art or full feature set.
