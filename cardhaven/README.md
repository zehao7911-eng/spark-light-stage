# Cardhaven
An original HTML5 card village inspired by the harvesting, stacking and crafting loop of Stacklands. All illustrations, interface and sounds are created in code; no external assets or libraries.

## Play
Drag a top card onto another card. Villager + Berry Bush / Tree / Rock yields Berries / Wood / Stone. Workers remain assigned until you move them. Worker + Wood / Stone processes materials into Planks / Bricks. Stack recipe ingredients to build; the Recipes book uses pictures. Drag spare cards to Sell. Resource nodes never run out. Your last essential resource node and all villagers cannot be sold.

10 guided quests, 27 illustrated cards, eight building/equipment recipes, cooking, smelting, recruitment (up to six villagers), equipment, ruin expeditions, voluntary monster battles, automatic sawmill/quarry production and repeating Beacon chapters. Three card pack catalogs rotate predictable contents rather than gambling. Sell resources, buy more materials, develop production and explore again.

At sunset villagers eat automatically. Berries provide one food; Stew provides three. Shortage slows work for 15 seconds and sends a Berry; villagers do not die. A free care parcel every 60 seconds restores missing essential nodes and workers, supplies food and emergency coins. Table capacity is 70 cards; production pauses at capacity. Sell surplus to resume.

## Controls and compatibility
Mouse or touch to drag. Drag empty tabletop to scroll, or use a mouse wheel. Hold a dragged card near the top/bottom edge to scroll while dragging. Arrange groups cards without overlap. Pause, speed (0.5×/1×/2×), mute and recipes controls. Local progress automatically saves; backgrounding pauses. Reset requires two taps. English only. Responsive portrait/landscape; device pixel ratio is capped. No real-money features. No network dependency. Open index.html directly for offline play, or serve this folder.

The ZIP includes a standalone index.html plus editable source (art.js, game.js, style.css, icon.svg).
