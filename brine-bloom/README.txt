BRINE & BLOOM
An original HTML5 micro-city building game, inspired by the accessible placement strategy of ISLANDERS. All buildings, terrain, animation, music tones and interface artwork are original code-drawn assets. No reference-game assets are included.

Play: choose a building card, tap an empty plot, inspect its score, then press Build. Nearby features reward or penalize placement. Reaching delivery milestones offers a choice of building packs. Coast-only structures must be placed at the edge. A neighborhood bonus rewards high-scoring placements.

You can undo your last placement before choosing a delivery. If supplies run out, neighbors send three spare buildings. Reaching the island target or filling every plot lets you sail onward. Four environmental palettes cycle across seeded, changing terrain. Completed islands enter the atlas; the newest 24 are kept locally. View any saved island and export a postcard PNG. Progress and current inventory save locally. No timers, payments or external downloads.

Use + and - to zoom. Drag the island when zoomed in. Portrait, landscape, desktop and touchscreen are supported. Sound begins after a gesture. Open index.html in a browser; no build step or server dependency is needed.
