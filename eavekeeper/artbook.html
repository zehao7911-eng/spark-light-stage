DESIGN SAMPLES · NOT EARNED GAME PROGRESS

A little roof. A living city.

Original flat-colour architecture, tiny geometric travellers and physical rain. The samples use exactly the same scene, clothing, roof and prop paths as the game, with authored sample designs. No commercial assets or generated images.

One shape language.

Arch windows repeat across buildings, bell housings, map cards and the icon. Flat lit and shadow planes define height. Small raincoat figures remain separate from distant architecture. Bright rose lines belong to the player's shelter; blue drops carry moving physics; gold marks carry reward. Sidewind previews are honest launch paths. Drawing and UI keep room for one-finger touch controls.
