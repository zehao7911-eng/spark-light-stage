# Ochre Keep

A compact, original HTML5 kingdom defense game inspired by [Thronefall](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2239150/Thronefall/). Its official day/night screenshots informed the faceted landscapes, cream masonry, blue roofs, small mounted king, long shadows and readable combat silhouettes. All geometry, levels, rules, interfaces and synthesized sounds here are original. This is a smaller browser interpretation, not the commercial game's assets or complete campaign.

## Play

Open `index.html`, or the self-contained `OCHRE-KEEP-OFFLINE.html`. No CDN, account, installation or server required. WebGL is required.

Day: tap a circular plot, then build. Windmills generate dawn income. Towers shoot automatically. Palisades hold enemies. Barracks recruit a squad each night. Forge levels improve king and guard damage. Buildings have three tiers and can be repaired.

Night: drag the joystick or drag over the ground to ride; desktop WASD or arrow keys also work. The king automatically attacks nearby enemies. Charge with the lightning button or Space. Flag/F toggles guard follow/hold. Defeat all attackers to receive income. Protect the central keep.

Six nights introduce raiders, archers, brutes, attacks from three directions and a siege lord. Royal gifts at dawn add income, a longbow, or fortifications. Winning unlocks continuing, increasingly difficult waves. Records and complete kingdom state save locally. Failure offers a retry from the last pre-battle day. A new kingdom preserves best-night and crown records.

English UI; portrait and landscape touch; 44px minimum buttons; 30fps rendering and DPR1.5; batched static scenery; paused/hidden render suspension; bounded effects. Audio starts only with a user gesture and can be muted. Three.js r147 bundled under MIT (see THREE-LICENSE.txt). No image generation or commercial assets.
