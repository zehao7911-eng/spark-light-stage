# Orbit Nursery
Original mobile HTML5 alien nursery incremental game. Inspired by the grow/sell/keep economy of Chillquarium, with original vector-style cosmic artwork and twelve animated alien designs.

Two free starters begin your nursery. Hold FEED to drop food; companions chase it and grow. Mature creatures produce energy each second. Keep them for ongoing income, or SEND HOME for an immediate reward. Tap a companion and mark KEEP to protect it from dispatch. Buy three-egg packs from four worlds. Expand the nursery to unlock each world and add room. Upgrade Superfood and automatic feeder bots. Shiny variants earn more and look golden. An empty station with insufficient energy can request a free rescue egg, preventing a resource deadlock.

English UI. Touch and mouse controls. Mobile portrait/landscape and desktop layouts. Progress, favorites and growing companions save locally. Hidden tabs and dialogs pause; no offline production claimed. No external libraries or assets. Packaged index.html is self-contained. Editable sources included. Serve on GitHub Pages or any static host. Sound begins after interaction and can be muted.
