FUSE & FEATHER
An original HTML5 bomb-stacking chicken escape, inspired by the central mechanic of Bomb Chicken (Nitrome).

PLAY
Open index.html in any modern browser. No installer, download, WebGL, CDN, account or network connection is needed after downloading the archive.
Touch: hold the left/right buttons to walk; hold STACK to lay a tower; KICK sends a bomb in the direction you face. Multiple fingers work together.
Keyboard: A/D or Left/Right; Space to stack; X to kick; Escape to pause.
Find the little chick, then enter the green exit. Collect the three optional gems. Blast wooden boxes, robots and round wall switches. Stone protects you from explosions. Bomb sides are forgiving to let you escape stacks; their tops support the chicken.

EIGHT ROOMS
Different platform spacing/heights, destructible packing crates, mandatory blast switches, chain-reaction barrels, conveyor belts, patrol robots, a high roost and a two-switch finale.
No timer or life limit. A tumble returns you to the start, or the rescued chick's checkpoint. Collected gems, opened gates and destroyed objects stay collected/open/destroyed.
Earn up to three badges per room: escape, all gems, no tumbles. Best badges never duplicate. Improve best times and unlock Biscuit/Mint feathers at 8/18 badges. Room book lets you revisit unlocked rooms. These are eight authored rooms, not an infinite generated campaign.

MOBILE / TECHNICAL
English interface, safe-area aware 44px+ buttons, responsive portrait and landscape, native Pointer Events, keyboard, capped visual effects and voice count, cached procedural Canvas 2D scenery. No GPU/WebGL dependency.
Fixed 60 Hz simulation; cached original pixel artwork. Synthesized audio begins only after interaction and can be muted. Pause, blur and hidden tabs freeze gameplay and stop sound. Local progress is saved automatically; active saves reopen behind a Continue screen. Private browsing/storage restrictions can prevent persistence without blocking gameplay.
Save postcard downloads a PNG. Source modules are included; the main index.html is also entirely self-contained.
