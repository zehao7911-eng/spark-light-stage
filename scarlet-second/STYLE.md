# Ink, velvet, a stolen second

The shared visual grammar is a near-black shape supporting a warm or desaturated face, not a dark filter over smooth primitives. Mask facets, coat folds, curtain folds, stone cracks, clock teeth, bell rims, and action shards all use angular connected shapes. Hatching follows the surface. Background hatching is sparse and lower contrast than actors.

Palette: ink #0c1215; stone #3b4947; porcelain #e8d7ad; old gold #d7af67; hostile scarlet #cf5a4d; grey steel #788c8c. Act I is cool green stone and maroon velvet. Act II adds tarnished brass. Act III shifts the stone to slate violet. Red always means an incoming returnable shot, gold always means a successful return or bell. The player has a pale split mask, feathered cap, sage coat and a tiny gold catching glove; enemies have distinct head silhouettes and charge bars.

At 400×560 logical scene units: courier around 36×65, ordinary enemies 44×65, king 58×85, bell 28×42. A frozen bullet has a 30–34 unit visible targeting ring and a 58–74 unit touch diameter. Objects sort by floor anchor. Floor perspective changes texture density without altering logical interaction coordinates. Cover has a visible base matching its collision footprint.

UI uses the same ink panels, old-gold ruled borders, cream lettering, serif display hierarchy, and restrained system labels. It does not use emoji as character art. First-run instructions are three short physical actions; later rooms add one feature at a time. Completion and upgrade choices are genuine game state.

Motion: enemy charge → scarlet diamond muzzle flash / red shot → lifted-finger tableau → bright gold conversion and short radial shards → fast homing return → armour chip or broken mask → room clears, stars and a concrete unlock. Small shake is capped and can be disabled. Particles never replace the character or objective. Cinematic idle candle motion is decorative; hostile simulation obeys the time rule.

The artbook renders the same original drawing functions used in gameplay: far theatre, close mask/bell/stone, character variants, shared palette and UI. It is a labelled art-direction fixture, not proof of gameplay or fabricated progress.
