PANIC PATROL
No spider left behind.

Open index.html in a recent browser. The packaged index is self-contained:
no CDN, account, model downloads or external assets are needed.
Editable scripts and styles are supplied alongside it.

Tap searchable clutter to flip it up, open it or toss it. Spiders burst out,
scuttle, jump and eventually find new hiding places. SCAN points to a hidden
pest and moves the camera toward its hiding place. Tap again to flush it out.

SWATTER: tap pests or make a quick swipe. Armored pests take two hits.
VACUUM: hold near a pest to latch on and pull it into the nozzle.
FROST: hold to freeze a pest, then switch to the swatter.
FIRE: hold to scorch pests and clutter. Burning objects reveal hidden pests.
Release powered tools to cool down. Overheated tools need a short rest.
Tools unlock as rooms open: vacuum in room2, frost in room3, fire in room4.

Drag slowly across empty space, or hold the left/right arrow buttons, to
look around. With a powered tool, dragging from a target follows your aim.
Desktop: A/D or arrows to look, 1-4 to select tools, Space to scan,
Escape to pause. Mouse taps/swipes/holds work like touch.

Six original rooms: living room, kitchen, bathroom, artist workshop,
greenhouse and bedroom. Each fresh hunt reshuffles hiding places.
Four pest types: runner, jumper, armored and gold. Six completed rooms
unlock after-hours calls which repeat the rooms with denser infestations.
They are remixed rooms, not an unlimited authored campaign.

Three badges per room: catch all pests; find all three stickers in clutter;
miss no more than eight swats. Maximum eighteen permanent badges.
Breaking a sticker-containing object also reveals its sticker.
There are no lives, failure countdowns or paid rewards.

The exact current hunt and permanent unlocks save locally in your browser.
Loading a hunt requires Continue hunt. Hiding the page or losing focus
pauses it. Fresh infestation replaces the hunt but retains earned badges.
Save job card downloads a PNG postcard. Audio begins after interaction.

Mobile portrait and landscape layouts, safe-area padding, 44px controls,
DPR capped at1.5, 30fps drawing, fixed60Hz simulation, batched room meshes,
instanced spiders/fire/debris and bounded voices/effects.
If WebGL fails or is lost, a simpler Canvas compatibility view preserves
the same game. Add ?flat=1 to select it. Mobile emulation is tested; actual
performance depends on your phone/browser.

Original compact browser interpretation inspired by Kill It With Fire.
This is not the full commercial game, its assets, story or campaign.
Original procedural models, room art, interface and synthesized audio;
no image generation. Three.js r147 is bundled under its MIT license.
