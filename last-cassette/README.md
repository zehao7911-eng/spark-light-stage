# LAST CASSETTE

An original, compact HTML5 road-trip RPG inspired by Keep Driving's side-on pixel landscapes, analogue dashboard, branching trips, hitchhikers and symbolic road challenges. All graphics and audio here are original code-generated work; no commercial assets are included. This is a smaller browser interpretation, not the full commercial game.

Open index.html directly, or serve this folder with any static server. No CDN, network requests, build tools or external dependencies are needed. GitHub Pages works under a subdirectory.

## Play

Choose a destination, then take six roads. Each route displays fuel use, wear, weather and its reward before departure. Road encounters pause automatically. Match Grip, Focus, Care and Heart symbols with skills; each driver skill can be used twice per encounter. Friends have one stronger skill per encounter. Tool rolls, coffee and snacks are consumed when used. A longer detour is always available.

At every stop you can refuel, repair, rest for free, buy supplies or work once for cash. Recruit up to two passengers, or say goodbye to free a seat. Fuel, energy and condition reaching zero at arrival ends the trip with a rescue ending. Different destinations yield different album stamps and paint colours. Starting a new trip changes routes and encounters; it keeps your album.

The journal records stops and encounters. Current travel progress, crew, cash, items, road challenges, paint and album save locally. Sound starts only after tapping Sound or the cassette display. Three original synthesized mixtapes. Pause with the dashboard button or Escape. Space opens routes at a stop or skips ahead on the road. A hidden browser tab automatically pauses.

Touch targets are at least 44 CSS pixels. Portrait and landscape layouts supported. The game uses a 720x360 pixel canvas, cached scenery and a 30 FPS art update. Keyboard, mouse and native touch input supported. Save data is stored only in your browser.

Reference: https://store.steampowered.com/app/2756920/Keep_Driving/

Emergency roadside assistance is available when fuel is below 12 or car condition below 10. It costs up to $10 and one day, so an empty wallet cannot strand you at a stop. Scenic routes add illustrated regional postcards to the persistent journal.
