# Tidelight Salvage

An original, dependency-free HTML5 incremental salvage game.

Serve this folder over HTTP, or upload its contents to GitHub Pages. Open index.html. Hold and sweep to cut debris; spend recovered metal in Workshop. Restore twelve wrecks, collect twelve relics and relight the coast. Deepwater expeditions retain equipment and collection.

Mobile portrait and landscape, mouse and touch. Synthesized sound activates on first interaction. Progress is stored locally; harbor income accrues for up to eight hours away. No external assets, requests, accounts or purchases.
