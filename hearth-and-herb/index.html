THE KETTLE CAN WAIT

Take a little pause.

Drag a herb into the mortar. Rub back and forth to grind it, then drag the powder into the pot.

Grind more finely for a longer path. Stir in circles over the pot, or hold STIR. Follow the dotted route on the map.

Near an effect, pump the bellows until the fire is ready. Bottle the remedy, then drag its bottle to your customer. Exact alignment earns three stars.

Tap controls also work: tap a herb, tap the mortar to grind, then tap again when fine. Tap a bottle and then the customer to sell. Keys 1–4 pick herbs; G grinds, Space stirs, F pumps, B bottles, Enter serves.

There is no hard time limit. Sleepy customers tip less. Herbs regrow, and shelves can hold three bottles. Saves are automatic.
