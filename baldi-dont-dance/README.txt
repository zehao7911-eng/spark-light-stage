BALDI — DON'T DANCE!
Hold to make the class dance. Release when the teacher starts turning.
Fill the chaos bar to make the teacher join. Each class gets a little faster.
Desktop: Space to dance, Escape to pause. Touch: hold the scene or bottom button.
The amber turn and descending warning tone tell you to release; the red eye means stay still.
Auto-saves cleared classes and best chaos. Interrupted classes restart on reload.
No money, accounts, ads, downloads or external network requests are required.
Open index.html in a browser with WebGL support. GitHub Pages also works.
Muted and hidden pages stop audio. Paused pages freeze the game. Optional vibration is used on supported browsers.
Photo button downloads an actual screenshot; it does not upload or share automatically.
