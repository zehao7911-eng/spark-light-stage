# AFTERHOURS — art direction and generation record

Mode: builtin OpenAI imagegen, text-to-image, no reference images. Generated and inspected October 26, 2026. Both selected final assets are saved locally in assets/city.webp and assets/actors.webp. Original generated PNG files are preserved under work/afterhours-study-art-source/ in the workspace. The frame metadata changes sampling bounds only; it does not paint over generated pixels. Background conversion is WebP quality 96; sprite atlas is lossless WebP and retains alpha.

## Shared visual rules

- Mature late-night city, front-facing facades and a horizontal road; human commuters and a believable old station wagon.
- Ink #111d31, deep blue #20394a, wet teal #45616a, fog #87a5a0, amber #e7c391, coral #c37876, neon #76c6c5.
- Fine grouped pixel materials, chrome highlights, stocked shop shelves, wet glass and pavement. Foreground reflections have lower opacity than the subjects.
- City/background and actors are separate files. Runtime adds human/traffic motion, camera parallax, headlight cones, brake-light glow, reflected sprites, rain, tire spray and muted optional sound.
- Generated atlas rows did not exactly match a uniform grid. Actual row boundaries were inspected, and foreground components were measured per cell. Cars share a union rectangle to preserve wheel/body alignment when brakes switch. No adjacent pedestrian/prop sprite bleeds into car frames.
