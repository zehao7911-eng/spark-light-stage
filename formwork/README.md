# FORMWORK — Orbital fabrication

A self-contained HTML5 factory-building game with original 3D machine models and synthesized sound. Visually references shapez 2's real official Steam screenshots: navy orbital grid, grey modular decks, ivory belt rails, dark moving treads, amber open gantries, teal paint nozzles and visible coloured quarter-shapes. This is an original smaller browser interpretation, not the commercial game's artwork or complete infinite-scale factory.

## Play
Drag belts to draw routes; choose a machine and tap a tile. The arrow rotates the next machine. Source extractors and the hub are permanent. Buildings are free and sources are infinite. There are no enemies, timers or build penalties. Zoom with buttons or pinch; use Move view to pan, or begin dragging on empty space. The view button rotates the isometric camera. Cutters output right halves forward and left halves to their left if a belt is attached, otherwise recycle offcuts. Rotators rotate clockwise. Painters use the selected swatch. Stackers take the base from behind and the top from the left; both feeds are needed. Splitters alternate forward/right, with fallback when one outlet blocks. Lifts change the outgoing belt's layer; build a lift-up on L1 and lift-down on L2.

Ten teaching contracts unlock progressively: round shapes, squares, halves, quarters, blue circles, pink halves, two layers, mixed shapes, precision quarters, and bridge experiments. Later open contracts vary shape, half/full, colour and stacked layers. Belts carry actual individual items with backpressure; machines wait when their outlets are blocked. Contract shape equality includes quadrant positions, colours, layers and layer order. Incorrect items are recycled without losing progress. First-time contract rewards buy four drive upgrades. Machine layouts remain between contracts. Guide outlines show a valid example without placing machines. Undo/redo preserves delivered progress and resets restored flow. Three local blueprint slots, contract gallery, pause, 1×/2×/3× speed, audio toggle and full current-factory saving are included.

Keyboard: 1 belt, 2 cutter, 3 rotator, 4 painter, 5 stacker; R turns the next machine; Space pause; Ctrl+Z undo / Ctrl+Shift+Z redo; Delete erase; Escape menu. Save key `formwork-v1`. No offline production; hidden tabs pause. Storage-unavailable mode remains playable. Extract ZIP and open index.html; keep all files together. Fully offline runtime, no CDN, fonts or account required. Three.js r147 is MIT licensed; see THREE-LICENSE.txt. Native WebGL-capable browser required. Two construction layers, compact 14×10 deck and scoped machine roster; no space-train system or commercial game's huge multi-platform universe.

Reference: https://store.steampowered.com/app/2162800/shapez_2/ . Reference screenshots were inspected during development and are not packaged.
