PEEL & PARCEL — Small stickers, big smiles

An original HTML5 sticker postcard shop inspired by the creative shop direction of Sticky Business. All 24 die-cut sticker illustrations, customer portraits, stationery and synthesized sounds are original; no reference-game assets are included.

Tap a catalog sticker to add it to the paper. Drag stickers to arrange them, then use Rotate or Remove on the selected sticker. Match the customer's two or three requested pictures; extra decoration is welcome. Some customers offer an optional bonus for a side-by-side pair, a stacked pair, or a third decoration. Paper cycles through four colors. Keep a little space between stickers for better tips.
Print your sheet, then sweep the roller across its entire width. Hold the wax seal or tap Seal & send. Each delivered order earns coins and a one-to-three-star layout rating. Buy three further sets of six stickers, pearl film and gold foil. Customers and requests keep changing without a time limit.
The album keeps the newest 16 designs and can export each as PNG. Coins, collections, orders and your current arrangement save locally. Reloading during printing/packing restores the arrangement to the design stage so it can be printed again; no completed-order rewards are duplicated.

Open index.html in a modern browser. No build system, external assets, fonts, CDN or network are required. Portrait, landscape and touch are supported. Audio begins after interaction; the game pauses when hidden.
