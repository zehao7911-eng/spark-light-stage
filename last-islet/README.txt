LAST ISLET — Hold the shore

English HTML5 real-time squad tactics. A compact original adaptation inspired by Bad North: Jotunn Edition's squad orders, island silhouettes, pale cliffs, houses, colored banners and incoming longboats. Models, island layouts, audio and game content are original and built locally. This is not the commercial game or its complete content.

HOW TO PLAY
Select Rowan, Freya or Asta, then tap a grass tile. Soldiers fight automatically. Prepare before sounding the horn. Red shore rings indicate incoming landings. Shield guards resist arrows, stationary pikes have long reach, archers attack from a distance but struggle against shields. High ground grants extra damage.

Each squad has a recharging ability. Replenish once per island: the squad moves beside a surviving house, then shelters for five seconds. It cannot accept move orders while replenishing. Save houses for gold, spend gold on three upgrade levels, or recruit replacements for fallen commanders. A successful island refills surviving squads before departure. Complete eight islands, then continue into an endless expedition with gradually stronger invaders. Failed islands can be retried.

CONTROLS
Touch or mouse: select squad, tap tile. Camera arrows rotate the island to reach obscured tiles. Pause permits move orders. Speed button selects 1x or 2x. Selecting briefly slows combat. Keyboard 1/2/3 selects squads, Space pauses. Sound requires interaction.

SAVING
Progress saves before island departure and after victory/purchases. Refresh during combat restarts that island from the departure checkpoint. Rewards are recorded once. Saves stay in the current browser. Begin expedition replaces your saved run. Hidden tabs pause simulation.

RUNNING
Serve the entire folder from a static HTTP/HTTPS host (GitHub Pages, or python -m http.server). No runtime CDN, fonts, account or network services. Three.js is included locally with its MIT license in vendor/LICENSE.txt. Requires a browser with WebGL enabled. Portrait and landscape touch layouts, capped pixel ratio, batched low-poly meshes and a single shadow map support mobile browsers; device performance can vary.

CONTENT
Eight island silhouettes and elevations; three squads with nine visible soldiers each, three upgrade levels and three abilities; three homes per island; raiders, shielded raiders, archers and axe leaders; mixed escort landings, three to five waves, saved-home income, recruitment, camp upgrades, campaign finale and endless expedition.
