# THE BRASS CHAMBER — A Mechanical Secret

A complete original handcrafted HTML5 puzzle casket, visually and mechanically informed by The Room's official Steam gallery: dark walnut, aged brass, concentric engraved plates, a dim teal study, warm desk light and inspecting a tactile layered box. No commercial models, textures, maps, music or puzzles were extracted. This is a small browser interpretation, not an identical copy of The Room.

Six linked discoveries: hold the nameplate screw to collect a revealing lens; read its hidden celestial inscription; set the front rings to open the key drawer; insert the key and align sliding pins; fit a cog and wake the resonance grid; fit the astral seal, align its orbits and hold the final release to open the hinged lid. The casket has four examinable faces and a top. Clues are on the object; journal records discoveries. Optional explicit hints prevent dead ends. Used items remain visible in inventory.

Mouse/touch: drag the scene to orbit, wheel/pinch to zoom, tap a brass marker to inspect. Arrow buttons turn to another face. Up arrow looks at the lid. L toggles collected lens; J opens journal. All controls work with large DOM targets; hold interactions also support keyboard Space/Enter. English UI; portrait and landscape. No countdown or loss. Discoveries and puzzle positions autosave locally, with a confirmed fresh-start action. Time on the ending screen is informational only.

Procedural wood/patina/wallpaper, detailed three-dimensional straps, screws, cogwork and ornaments; one 1024px soft shadow light, painted environment reflections, lamp and moon fill, floating dust and a lit faceted core. Static meshes are batched; DPR capped at 1.6. No external requests, CDN, online account or server required. WebGL needed. Audio begins after interaction and can be muted. Physical phone hardware performance is not yet measured.

ZIP root index.html is self-contained. Open it directly or host statically. Editable art.js, game.js and style.css are included. three.min.js is Three.js r147 under MIT; the embedded license is retained.

Scope: one authored casket with six linked stages, not the full commercial campaign. It emphasizes a polished playable visual sample. No random filler levels are included.
