# Artwork and animation sources

Reference: https://rezona.ai/game/pgcserver/pv/LMfXptZecQ/v9/dist/index.html

Fourteen selected public images: one illustrated lawn, five plant cutouts, four undead portraits and four 2×2 walking sheets. The normal sheets are512×512; the boss sheet is768×768. Four walking poses are cropped at render time from the supplied grids. Plants use independent recoil/breathing transforms and attack effects written for this game. No claim is made that those are newly drawn poses or 3D models.

Each exact source URL, source SHA-256, dimensions and runtime WebP SHA-256 appears in assets/PROVENANCE.json. Unmodified source masters are in assets/originals. Compressed WebP derivatives and tiny embedded previews in bootstrap.mjs keep the first display independent of all full images arriving. Cover and icon compose credited runtime cutouts/background with original title lettering and framing. No image-generation tool was used.
