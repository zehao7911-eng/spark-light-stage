# Moonpost

An original p5.js miniature pinball delivery adventure inspired by the pinball-adventure idea in Yoku's Island Express. No commercial artwork, maps, audio, or code is included. Eight original pocket tables, four night palettes, a clockwork lunar post office, planet bumpers, moving mail stations, rotating relay bars, combination chimes, three collectible stars per table, a delivery journal, unlockable courier colors and PNG postcards.

Press Launch. Hold the left/right buttons independently to operate the flippers; both fingers can play at once. Keyboard: A/D or arrow keys, Space to launch/nudge, Escape to pause. Light every mail station: each needs the indicated number of hits. Once ready, choose POST or keep playing for the three optional stars. Collect all three with all mail ready to finish automatically. Nudge lifts a low ball and steers toward the center, with a three-second recharge. Missing a ball safely resets the launch and keeps all mail and star progress. No lives or timer.

Stars reward delivery, all three collectibles, and at most two ball rescues. Best ratings are capped at 24; replays cannot farm stars. Six stars unlock Mint, fifteen unlock Rose. Scores have per-table records. Active position, velocity, random state, moving-station phase, flipper angles and progress are saved; reload opens paused and clears held inputs. Blocked storage allows session play. Pause also freezes effects and stops audio. Postcards export the actual completed scene.

Open index.html directly or serve this folder. No build, WebGL, network request or account required. Original code-drawn Canvas 2D art, bundled p5.js 1.11.11 (LGPL-2.1, see P5-LICENSE.txt), fixed 120 Hz simulation, 45 FPS drawing, capped 1.5 pixel density, 100 visual events, 16 trail points and 14 audio voices. Native pointer input, responsive portrait/landscape letterboxing, 44px or larger controls and safe areas. Phone browser emulation does not replace physical-device testing.
