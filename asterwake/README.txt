ASTERWAKE · THE LUMINOUS GARDENS
Original HTML5 action game inspired by the approachable ship controls of Minishoot' Adventures and the evolving weapon builds of Nova Drift. No art or audio from those games is included.
Open index.html in a current browser. All graphics and audio are generated locally. No CDN, login, server or network dependency.
Drag anywhere to steer, or use WASD / arrow keys. Firing and aiming are automatic. Tap the star button or press Space to dash, become briefly invulnerable and clear nearby projectiles.
Break eight crystal seals, collect light, choose visible weapon evolutions and defeat the guardian. Fly onward with your current weapon into increasingly challenging gardens. Three original painted color themes rotate.
Light is banked on victory or defeat. Permanent hull, engine and collector upgrades apply across flights. A restored garden can be exported as a PNG postcard.
The flight and workshop save on this device. Refresh then Resume flight. Menus and hidden tabs pause gameplay. Audio starts only after interaction.
English UI. Touch and keyboard controls. Canvas pixel ratio is capped; particles, enemies and projectiles are bounded.
