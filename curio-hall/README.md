# Curio Hall

An original small HTML5 museum management game, inspired by Two Point Museum's exhibit curation, expeditions, guest observation and themed presentation. The reference appeared in Steam's global live top sellers when researched on October 11, 2026. This is a compact original adaptation, not a reproduction of the commercial game's content, characters or 3D assets. All graphics and synthesized sound are made in code. No generated images, external libraries, remote assets, build process, accounts or purchases.

Start with a longneck skeleton and an ammonite. Send expeditions to Fossil Coast, Kelp Shelf and Starfall Ridge to discover all twelve original curiosities. Each expedition guarantees a new discovery. New regions unlock at three and six collected finds. Place or move finds into nine exhibition spaces for free. Placing over an occupied space swaps two displayed finds, or returns the displaced find to storage. A find can only exist on one display. Storing and moving preserves improvements. Upgrade twice for better donations, greenery, ropes and a masterpiece rating. Matching orthogonal neighbours give 20% more appeal each; theme diversity adds 5% per theme. Polish worn displays for free. Guests pay admission, walk around actual exhibit footprints, react, take photographs and donate before leaving. Guided tours boost donations 70% for twelve simulation seconds, with a thirty-second cooldown. Six curator goals reward coins, stars and two hall colour schemes. Twelve discoveries and the final goal lead to a collection ending; the hall stays playable afterwards.

Tap a display for its options. Tap a space to choose a find, or choose Finds and then a space. Album shows your collection. Pause contains pace (1x/2x), sound and hall colours. Escape cancels placement or closes a menu. Menus and hidden tabs pause both visitors and expeditions. There are no loss conditions or spending traps: collected exhibits can always be placed for free and admissions/donations provide income. Save includes discoveries, cash, upgrades, stored exhibits, expedition progress, goals, time and hall palette. Guests restart on load. There is no offline income or offline expedition progress. Local storage failure never prevents play.

Open index.html directly in a modern browser or publish this folder on a static HTTPS host such as GitHub Pages. English UI. Responsive portrait/landscape, semantic buttons, keyboard focus, minimum 44px targets, reduced motion, mute and automatic save. Canvas2D with DPR capped at 1.5, rendering capped at 35fps, fixed 20Hz simulation, at most 18 visitors and 70 bounded feedback effects. Browser viewport and pointer testing is not a physical handset performance test.
