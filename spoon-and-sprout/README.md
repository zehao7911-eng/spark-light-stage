# Spoon & Sprout — continuous-field edition
Original HTML5 incremental excavation game, revised around the shop, dirt currency, autonomous tools, treasure inventory and skill-tree structure demonstrated on the official Dirt Clicker Steam page. Original artwork and independently implemented rules; not the official Dirt Clicker game.

Hold or drag on the terrain to dig. Space also digs. Soil yields dirt currency. Buy multiple spoons, shovels and pickaxes: each tool moves and digs automatically. Hire mushroom helpers, golems and druids. Treasures stay in INVENTORY until you sell one type or SELL TREASURES. SKILL TREE has prerequisite branches for manual power, speed, area, automatic tool power and treasure luck. Clear 70% to advance, 90% to uncover a new region relic; full clearing gives a larger bonus. Six region palettes and eighteen collectible treasure types. Progress saves locally; previous edition progress is retained, while its small field is replaced by the continuous-field map. Mobile portrait stacks panels; landscape and desktop show the shop alongside the field.

No external dependencies or network assets. Packaged index.html is self-contained. Editable source files are included. Audio starts after interaction. Hidden pages pause; no offline income is claimed.
