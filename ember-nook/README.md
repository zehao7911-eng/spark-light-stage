# Ember Nook

An original fireplace toy adventure inspired by the playful combustion and combo discoveries of Little Inferno. This is an independent compact browser game, with original illustrations, code and synthesized sound. It includes no commercial game assets, external libraries, generated images, advertisements or purchases.

Open index.html directly, or put the six files on any static web host.

Choose the two toys shown on a request card and tap Ignite. You can also drag toys from the shelf into the hearth, drag existing toys to stack or toss them, and tap a toy to ignite it. Burning objects spread heat to nearby toys. Combos trigger when the corresponding two kinds ignite within 3.5 seconds. Requested pairs are always visible. There is no loss timer, limited stock, currency cost to experiment or game-over.

Eight parcels introduce twelve recipes and eight toys. The first four toys are available immediately; finishing the first parcel opens the other four. Corn pops, rockets jump and ignite nearby objects, robots kick surrounding toys, and music boxes chime. Boats become paper birds, moons and flowers scatter stars, and cups produce bubbles. The fireplace has actual gravity, mass-weighted soft collisions, accumulating ash, spreading heat and bounded effects.

Stars: finish the requested combos; use no more than the displayed result's target (2 per requested pair plus 3 spare toys); chain at least as many distinct combo discoveries as there are request cards, within five seconds of one another. You may take as long as you like to prepare; there is no timed gold requirement. A finished order waits 3.3 seconds so toys can react, then awards embers and stars. First clears award more embers; replays award two plus any improved star record. Embers purchase permanent Brass, Porcelain and Snow fireplace decorations in the collection. Twelve discoveries and best stars persist. After eight parcels, Afterglow repeats the eight request sets, with no claim of infinitely unique levels.

Keyboard: 1–8 choose toys; Space ignites; Escape pauses. Standard Tab/Enter works on shelf and UI buttons. Sound begins after a user gesture. Pause, blur and hidden tabs suspend audio and freeze the scene. Cancelled shelf drags create no toys. Cancelled in-hearth drags release safely without throwing or igniting. Reload restores the active fireplace with all objects released. Corrupt saves are rejected; unavailable storage falls back to session-only play.

Portrait/landscape layouts, safe-area padding, 44px UI controls and canvas DPR capped at 1.5 are included. Effects cap at 150, live toys at 16, ash at 24 and one-shot audio voices at 14. Save postcard downloads a PNG. No network requests are needed for offline play. Browser touch emulation is used for validation; physical handset performance requires testing on the actual device.
