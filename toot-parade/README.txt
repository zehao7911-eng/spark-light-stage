TOOT PARADE — an original little brass rhythm game

Hold the pitch rail on the right and slide up/down to match each approaching
note at the gold line. Touching the chart also controls pitch and breath.
Release to stop the horn; the separate HOLD TO TOOT button uses current pitch.
Notes with sloped ends need a smooth slide. Your own pitch makes the horn sound,
not a prerecorded melody. Bass/percussion accompaniment is synthesized locally.
Six original short melodies, four stage palettes, normal and inverted encores.
Feedback: Bravo/Nice/Wobbly/Honk, note sparks, comic player cheeks/tilt, crowd
cheers, instrument slide motion, combo and score. A missed note never ends a song.
Stars: finish,55% in tune,83% without Easy. Easy slows time to80% and widens
pitch tolerance; choose before starting or on a curtain-call screen.
First clear:6+2*stars brass; replay:2+2*stars. Four outfit/instrument styles cost
0/16/24/32 brass; permanently unlocked styles and best stars/accuracy are saved.
Songbook replays unlocked songs; replacing a live performance needs a second tap.
Automatic local save and Continue, paused chart and silent horn, persistent mute.
Refreshing releases breath. A completed save resumes its result without extra pay.
Keyboard:1..6 pitch positions,Up/Down fine tuning,hold Space to blow,P/Escape pause.
English UI; touch/mouse, portrait/landscape, p5 Canvas2D,DPR capped1.5,noCDN/WebGL.
Open index.html offline or upload this whole folder to any static host.
Private/blocked storage can disable saving; playable sound is started by your click.
Original code/cartoon drawings/compositions/WebAudio sounds, no generated images
or commercial assets. Reference: Trombone Champ, Holy Wow,Steam1059990. Inspected
its official gallery: moving pitch lines,golden horn,expressive player and feedback.
This compact original cartoon-stage interpretation does not include that game's
characters,songs,photographic backgrounds,3D player or complete content.
