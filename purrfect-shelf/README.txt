PURRFECT SHELF · A LITTLE ROOM OF YOUR OWN
Original HTML5 cozy organizing game inspired by the gentle everyday-object puzzles of A Little to the Left and the room-building satisfaction of Unpacking. No assets from those games are included.
Drag objects onto their faint matching outlines. You can also tap an object, then tap its outline. After planting the window garden, hold each plant to water it. After furnishing Miso's corner, tap the cat. Swipe the misty window during the rainy-day task.
Eight little tasks build one complete illustrated room: tea, books, plants, a cat corner, treats, pictures, window cleaning and a gift parcel. Completed objects stay in the room.
Earn four hearts per task and use them for wall colors, rug patterns and three cat coats. Finish all tasks to freely rearrange the room. Another cozy day changes the arrangement order and keeps your decorations and hearts.
Pet Miso any time. Save a PNG room postcard using Save room.
Everything saves locally on this browser. No external libraries, fonts, downloads, sign-in or server needed. Production and interactions stop while the page is hidden. Sound starts only after interaction.
English UI, portrait and landscape touch controls. All scenery, item illustrations, cat animation and synthesized audio are original.
