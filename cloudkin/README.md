# Cloudkin Isles

A gentle, original island adventure inspired by creature collecting in Moonstone Island and the pixel-art categories in My Game Art Style. This is a small original game, not a recreation of the original game or its assets.

Tap anywhere to walk; tap a spirit to make a friend. Meet six spirits to unlock three islands and fill the field guide. Tap glowing crystals to collect stardust. Progress is saved in the browser, including saves from the previous version.

Mouse/touch: tap to walk and use menus. Keyboard: WASD or arrow keys to move; use mouse for spirits and menus. Open index.html in a modern browser. Resources are local. Pixel scenery was generated for this game; characters, effects, and interface were created for the game.
