# AFTER HOURS — The Debt Club

A mobile-first HTML5 interpretation of Gamble With Your Friends. Official Steam screenshots and trailer informed the low-poly wide-eyed companions, dark patterned carpet, warm brass, magenta/cyan lighting, first-person casino and shared-bank debt loop. All scene meshes, patterns and sounds are newly drawn/generated in code. This is not the commercial game or its assets.

Walk around five machines: animated slots, red/black/green roulette, high/low/doubles dice, blackjack with ace handling and dealer strategy, and real-time rocket cash-out. All machines share one wallet. Five-minute quotas lead through four room palettes, then endless escalating days. Collect chips, bus tables for safe income, purchase consumable payout helpers, and unlock companion hats with persistent tickets. Three AI companions wander; Pip scavenges income without placing unauthorized automatic bets.

Controls: WASD/arrow movement, drag to look, E/use button to interact, map button for positions. Mobile: left joystick and right-side swipe, large panel buttons. Touch and keyboard input are independent. Gameplay pauses when the document is hidden and the pause menu is open. Active betting dialogs keep the round timer running. An unfinished hand cannot be abandoned for a refund. No money, account, payment, prizes or cash-out outside this fictional game.

Scope: solo + AI, not online multiplayer, voice chat, all 17 commercial mini-games or the full four authored commercial floors. Four palettes reuse one compact room layout. Tickets/best day save locally; active runs restart on reload. Needs WebGL. Procedural audio starts after interaction. No remote asset/CDN dependency.

Open index.html or host the directory statically. three.min.js is Three.js r144, MIT; its embedded license is retained. Editable game.js and style.css included.
