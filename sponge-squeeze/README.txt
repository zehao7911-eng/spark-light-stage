SPONGE SQUEEZE! — Unofficial SpongeBob SquarePants fan game
Drag SpongeBob sideways to aim at a Patrick. Pull down to squeeze out water. Release to soak up more water. Fill all three Patricks before the timer runs out; they inflate, burp and float away.
The top blue bar shows water left in the sponge. The three bars under Patrick show their fill. Later rounds need more water and the Patricks sway a little. No lives, purchases, ads or accounts.
Keyboard: Space squeezes; Left / Right aim left / right, Down aims middle. Release Space to refill. Escape pauses.
Cleared rounds, best streak and mute preference save locally. Reload restarts the active round. Hidden/blurred pages pause and silence audio. Resume explicitly. Optional vibration on supported browsers. Screenshot button saves a PNG locally and never uploads it.
Original procedural fan models, expressions, textures, animation, code and synthesized sounds. No ripped commercial meshes, recordings, music or textures included. SpongeBob SquarePants and Patrick Star belong to Nickelodeon / Paramount. Not an official product or the commercial SpongeBob game.
Reference characters: https://www.nick.com/info-page/xe31fl/spongebob-squarepants and https://www.nick.com/info-page/8hz5p5/patrick-star
Open index.html in Chrome, Safari or Edge with WebGL enabled. All assets are bundled into index.html; no CDN or runtime network requests. Portrait-first touch, landscape and keyboard supported. Three.js r147, MIT; see THREE-LICENSE.txt.
