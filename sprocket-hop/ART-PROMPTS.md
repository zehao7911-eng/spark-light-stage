# Image-generation provenance and final prompts

All assets used the built-in image_gen.imagegen tool, not the CLI. Final PNGs are included unchanged. Backgrounds and cover are opaque; the robot atlas retains actual alpha. No reference image was taken from another game. Image 1 below established this project's palette and materials.

## Robot atlas revision history

An initial atlas, exec-e254948d-3b0e-42bd-b227-99d273885921.png, established the character. It was discarded from the game after phone review because microdetail became noisy at runtime. It was a style/material reference generation based on daybreak-dock.png, not an imported character. Its prompt was:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: ONE transparent 2x2 animation-pose sprite atlas for SPROCKET HOP. Reference image is only pixel style/material/palette reference. Design ONE original adorable small service robot, then draw it in four distinct poses, same proportions/character/camera/pixel scale throughout. Rounded-square cream metal head, dark slate faceplate with two warm amber friendly eyes, short antenna with coral tip, teal padded torso, copper joint rings, two small mitten hands, two chunky magnetic boots and a small twin-nozzle jetpack. Three-quarter nearly-front camera, same angle in every pose, facing slightly right. Top-left quadrant: neutral standing idle, arms relaxed. Top-right: crouch/compressed anticipation pose, feet planted and knees bent. Bottom-left: airborne jumping with knees tucked and arms forward, TWO short tiny amber jet flames below the backpack. Bottom-right: standing and waving with one raised mitten, happy eyes. These are four frames of SAME robot, not four different robots. Each object centered in its own equal quadrant, generous transparent gutter, no object crossing boundaries, whole antenna and boots fully visible. At game size of about 28x36 pixels the silhouette and pose must remain legible. Clean sharp pixel clusters, tactile shaded ceramic/metal surfaces, cream/teal/copper/amber/slate palette coherent with reference, no painterly blur or vector rendering. ACTUAL transparent alpha background, no ground plane or shadow cloud, no checkerboard pattern, no labels, numbers, lettering, UI, logos or watermarks. Sprite atlas only.
