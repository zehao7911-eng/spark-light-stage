# HONK HOLLOW

A single-player village mischief sandbox for browsers, inspired by Untitled Goose Game's matte garden colors, white-goose silhouette, contextual carrying, honking and comic villager reactions. All procedural models, village layout, sound and code are original. This is a smaller browser adaptation, not the complete commercial game or its assets.

Walk using the on-screen joystick, WASD/arrows or ground taps. Tap a loose object to approach and pick it up. PICK UP/USE is contextual; DROP puts your carried object down. HONK startles nearby villagers and can knock the gardener's hat loose. SNEAK makes you slower and conceals you beside the hedges or reeds. Villagers react to noise, notice their possessions, chase you, take their property back, carry it home and restore it. Picking up a nearby villager's possession gives a short surprised pause before pursuit. The sprinkler attracts the gardener and showers him. The fruit cart releases three rolling apples. The painter repairs beak marks. The pond accepts a little boat.

Twelve persistent tasks: fright, hat theft, sprinkler shower, key picnic, four food/drink picnic items, scattered apples, stolen radio, village bell, hedge escape, a ball in the goal, a marked painting and a final golden bell. Objects left in the picnic basket are safe. Open the mischief list or tap the current task for an automatic walking route toward that place; you still perform the action. All areas are open from the start. Completion grants score/combos, a scarf at four tasks and a hat at eight. Eleven tasks reveal the golden bell; all twelve unlock free mischief. There is no death or timed failure. Villagers and toys remain active for repeat play. A fresh morning resets the tasks and scene while retaining earned costumes and best score.

Current goose position, carried item, loose items, secured picnic items, water/cart state, task completion, score, costume and sound save locally. NPC pursuit routes and transient animation do not save. Storage may be unavailable under restricted browser settings. Hidden tabs pause simulation and release inputs; pause and list also stop world simulation.

English UI, portrait and landscape touch controls. Local Three.js r147, MIT license included; procedural geometry and canvas sign/bubble textures, one 1024 shadow map, capped DPR1.5, instanced flower details. Requires WebGL. No external requests, fonts, commercial art or models. ZIP index.html is self-contained for offline use.
