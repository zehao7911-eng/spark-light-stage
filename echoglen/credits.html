A little help goes a long way.

Echoglen's nine-room campaign, echo puzzles, controller, renderer, UI and synthesized audio are original work.

Illustration

Three environments, a directional fox sheet and a nine-prop sheet were generated together using OpenAI's built-in ImageGen tool. The assets use a shared palette, camera and illustrated materials. Sprite registration and alpha bounds were measured individually.
