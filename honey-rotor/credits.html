Honey Rotor

A small-batch nectar puzzle. Turn the six-sided hive, connect three touching blocks of one color, and fill your honey jars. A complete 12-batch journey with three environments, score chains, a charged Honey Burst, three spill allowances, saved continuation and a collectible honey shelf.

Real open-source game adaptation

Play

Arrow keys or A/D rotate the hive. Space or Down pours the current block. S triggers a charged burst. On mobile, use the large buttons or swipe the hive. Adjacent means touching on one side or above/below; diagonal colors do not count. Blocks connect across the sixth and first side of the hive.

Each batch has a harvest goal. A stack reaching the dotted rim spills its outer layers and costs one heart. Three spills end the batch. Three stars mean no spills; two stars mean one spill; otherwise a completed batch gets one star. Best stars and scores are retained rather than added again. Nectar fills Burst; a full Burst clears up to two outside layers per stack. The starting batch gives an immediate match to teach the rule.

Sound starts off. Tap the music button to enable the gentle synthesized notes and interaction sounds. Pause and browser backgrounding freeze play and suspend sound. Progress is stored on this browser when storage is available. No online account is required.
