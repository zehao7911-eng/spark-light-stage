# Bramble & Broth
An original pixel cooking adventure for mobile and desktop.

Gather ingredients in three gardens. Return to the pantry, open the kitchen, tap guest orders to cook. Plate when the simmer meter reaches its golden zone, then tap the matching order to serve. Perfect bowls and streaks earn bonuses. Upgrade your ladle, backpack, coat and stoves to discover six recipes.

Touch: thumb stick, attack and dodge buttons. Desktop: WASD/arrows, Space attack, Shift dodge, E interact, R return. Progress saves locally. No external libraries or network assets. Audio starts after interaction. Extract the ZIP and host index.html on any static host. The packaged index is self-contained.

Original procedural pixel artwork and synthesized sound. Inspired by the exploration/cooking loop of Cuisineer; no game assets copied.
