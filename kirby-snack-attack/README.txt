KIRBY: SNACK ATTACK!
Unofficial fan mini-game. Kirby and Waddle Dee belong to Nintendo / HAL Laboratory, Inc.
Original procedural geometry, animation, code and synthesized sound. No extracted commercial assets.

Hold anywhere on the scene to inhale. Drag sideways to move and aim.
Let go to spit a star at the highlighted cabinet. More snacks = bigger stars.
Chili adds splash damage. Rubber duck adds a bounce to another cabinet.
Free all three Waddle Dees to advance. No timer, lives, payments or accounts.
Cabinets move gently in later rooms and gradually become stronger (capped at six hits).
Maximum belly: six units. Boots count double. Snacks replenish automatically.
Pause and pointer cancellation keep your snacks without firing a star.
Desktop: hold Space to inhale, arrows/A/D to move, release Space to spit. Escape pauses.
Room progression and mute preference save locally when browser storage is available.
SAVE THE CHAOS exports a PNG. The next room remains playable if storage is blocked.

Open index.html directly or host the folder on any static HTML5 host.
All scripts are bundled; no network dependencies, build step or installation.
Three.js r147 is bundled under the accompanying MIT license.
Design reference: the collection-to-growth satisfaction of Katamari; this is an original short fan mini-game, not a port.
