CROWN OF MIST
An original HTML5 pixel kingdom inspired by the riding, coin spending and night-defense loop of Kingdom Two Crowns. This is an independent single-player adaptation, not the commercial game.

CONTROLS
Touch: hold the left/right buttons to ride, use the coin button beside a building, rally to empower nearby archers.
Keyboard: arrows / A-D to ride, Space / Down to spend, R to rally, Escape to pause.

THE WATCH
Recruit wanderers at the forest camps, then equip them at the bow or hammer shop. The village holds 12 subjects, so balance archers and builders. Raise both walls and watchtowers. Builders repair damaged surviving walls by day. Farms and the keep improve your dawn income. Each dawn offers one permanent blessing. Treasure chests and the moonsteed lie beyond the borders. Defend eight nights to restore the valley, then continue the endless watch.
Enemy contact steals coins; contact with an empty purse loses your crown. Enemies that breach a wall can knock tools away from your subjects; equip them again at the shops. They can also destroy the beacon. The rally grants a brief protective shield and faster shooting to nearby archers. The game saves progress locally, including the current night. Hidden tabs pause simulation.

All art and audio are generated by the included code. No network dependencies. Extract the ZIP and open index.html, or host this folder on any static HTML5 host. No account, payment or external assets are required.
