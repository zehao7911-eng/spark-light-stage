# Neon Wardens

A complete six-mission original tactical HTML5 campaign, inspired by telegraphed-threat tactics in Into the Breach. Original isometric Canvas artwork, original characters and procedural audio. No external dependencies or assets.

## Play

Select a mech. Each can move once and strike once; striking ends its movement. Tap Move or Strike, tap a highlighted tile to preview, then tap the same tile again to confirm. Undo freely before ending the turn. Enemy arrows update after pushes and interruptions. Violet circles show next-turn emergence points; standing on one blocks it at a health cost.

- VANGUARD: adjacent 2-damage strike and push.
- LANCE: straight-line 1-damage strike and push, up to 4 tiles.
- ARC: target and cardinal neighbors, 1 damage and interrupt enemy attacks.

Push into water or off the board for an instant elimination. Collision damages both objects. Watch friendly fire and civilian buildings. Fire burns after the enemy attacks. Moths hover over water and attack all adjacent tiles. The final Leviathan must be defeated before the sixth turn ends.

## Campaign

Six authored districts introduce water, fire, reactors, crossfire, and a boss. Between missions, choose one of eight upgrades. Health repairs, city power, equipment and campaign progress carry over. Failures allow a mission retry or a fresh campaign. Victory unlocks endless patrol with additional enemies. The full campaign and current battle autosave locally.

## Controls and hosting

Touch or mouse. Desktop shortcuts: 1/2/3 squad, M move, A strike, Enter confirm, Z undo, Space enemy turn. Pause is available. Rendering suspends in hidden tabs. Sound activates after a gesture. Tested at mobile portrait, mobile landscape and desktop sizes.

Serve this folder over HTTP or publish on GitHub Pages. ZIP includes `index.html` at its root. No account, real money, or network connection is needed after loading the files.
