# Coastline Garage

A small original 3D HTML5 vehicle-restoration game inspired by the repair theme of Car Mechanic Simulator 2021. This is a simplified stylized game, not a recreation of the original's detailed vehicle systems or assets. The four jobs reuse the same vehicle model with different finished colors.

Choose Wheels, Engine, Lights or Paint. Press the labeled action button three times to loosen, remove and refit parts, or apply paint. The scene also has glowing clickable rings. Once all repairs are done, press Finish and watch the car depart; the next job unlocks. View switches sides. Progress in completed jobs saves in your browser; current unfinished repairs do not persist across refresh.

The game uses original procedural geometry and synthesized audio, with Three.js bundled under its MIT license in vendor/. No image generation or remote game assets are used. Mouse/touch; modern browser with WebGL required. Browser phone-sized layout and one complete job were tested, not physical devices.
