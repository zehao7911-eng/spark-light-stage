# A shelf of wind-up wonders

The style is rounded three-dimensional toy sculpture, not pixel art or the previous ink theatre. Shapes use continuous bevels, capsules and full curved silhouettes. Cream porcelain faces, muted coral fabric, teal painted tracks and small brass wind-up parts share one controlled palette. Detail is concentrated in faces, button holes, spool thread bands, winding keys, tiny feet and clean contact shadows. Repeated scenery is deliberately quieter than the active track.

Base palette: cream #fff1d5; coral #dc8064; teal #639f91; blue #63a7b5; brass #dda75e; ink #284c4e. The first workshop is mint/cream, Bubble Station shifts to rose/blue, and the last courses use slate blue and warm gold. Action meanings remain stable: coral low spool = hop, teal overhead arch = tuck, blue gap = jump, gold pad = automatic spring, gold button = optional route reward.

Pip is a plump original wind-up bird with a cream body, seafoam wings/bib, button eyes, peach beak and feet, single golden tuft, and brass winding key. Running alternates the feet and turns the key. Flight tilts the body; tucking compresses the figure with an actual matching lower collision height; the unlocked glider spreads two wings. Eye placement sits on the visible curved head surface. Background toys have different silhouettes from Pip and from obstacles.

The scene is a side-view miniature track seen through an orthographic camera with elevated depth. Fixed input buttons avoid perspective targeting problems on phones. The bird stays near the left quarter of the action field, leaving forward visibility. Foreground figures and spools sit below the track; the quieter back-row workshop skyline occupies the upper field. Tracks visibly break at gaps. Soft shadows and thick edges distinguish tabletop, road and props.

The interface uses cream rounded cards, deep green typography, coral HOP and seafoam TUCK buttons. Icons accompany the two short action words. A cue waits above the action field. Score feedback sits high in the view so it does not cover Pip. Completion leaves the scene visible and adds a compact reward card. Toybox removes gameplay counters and uses the same lighting and original toy models.

Audio is original synthesized foot taps, spring tones, collection notes, pin chords and impact noise. Sound is unlocked by a user gesture. Meshes, geometry and effects are bounded; DPR caps at 1.5, shadows use one 1024 map, and paused scenes are not repeatedly rendered. Gentle mode removes shadows and camera shake. No offline earnings, advertising, purchases or tracking.
