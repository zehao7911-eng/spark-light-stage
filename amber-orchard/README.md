# Amber Orchard

An original HTML5 incremental orchard, built with Canvas and synthesized audio. No external assets, fonts, libraries, tracking or payments.

Hold a tree crown and sweep between trees to shake down ripe fruit. Amber is credited immediately. Golden fruit grants nectar. Plant all six trees and reach the garden's harvest goal to open the next garden. A ready Breeze ripens and shakes every tree. Every thirty fruit triggers a Harvest Feast.

Grow six gardens, discover twelve fruit varieties, invite rabbit and hedgehog harvest friends, and improve the orchard through nine upgrade paths. New years preserve friends, equipment and discoveries, and improve amber earnings and growth. Progress saves automatically to this browser. Helpers earn amber for up to eight hours away.

Upload this folder to a static HTML host or serve it locally over HTTP. Entry point: index.html. Works with mouse, touch, portrait and landscape layouts. Sound starts only after a user gesture. Reduce-motion and reset controls are in Settings.

Inspired by the garden growth and helpers of Plantera 2: Golden Acorn; artwork, code, names and active harvest mechanics are original.
