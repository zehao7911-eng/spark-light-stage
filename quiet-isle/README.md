# THE QUIET ISLE

A small, original sunlit garden of sixteen line puzzles, inspired by the visual language and region-rule puzzles of The Witness. This is an independent browser interpretation, not the commercial game's assets, levels or complete open world.

## Play
Drag from the large circle to the open end, or tap adjacent nodes. Trace backwards to shorten the line. Clear and Undo are always available. Incorrect clues are circled without deleting your work. Hint suggests one next node using a local worker. LOOK lets you rotate the view; RETURN brings you back to the panel.

Four chapters introduce marked dots, separation of coloured squares, triangle edge counts, and pairs of stars. Mixed panels obey every displayed rule. Completing a panel powers a cable and opens a gate. Every fourth panel lights a garden beacon. All completed panels remain available from the garden map. Progress is saved locally when browser storage is available.

Keyboard: arrows start/draw, Backspace undo, Escape clear. No time limit, wagering, accounts or purchases. All interface text is English.

## Mobile and offline
Root index.html includes all JavaScript and CSS and opens without a server or CDN. The separate source files are included for editing. WebGL is required. Audio is synthesized locally after a user interaction. No network is required for art, hints, game logic or sound.

Touch tested in Chromium at 390x844 and landscape 844x390; desktop tested at 1440x900. These are browser emulation tests, not measurements on physical iOS/Android devices. Pixel ratio is capped at 1.5; foliage, grass and paving are instanced and stationary meshes batched. Approximately 35 scene draw calls. GPU/device/browser support still affects performance.
