# Snackdash City

An original browser arcade game inspired by the playful chase premise of Rezona's Fish Heist and the lively, colorful street-game aesthetic of OlliOlli World. This project does not include their characters or assets.

Jump over crates and puddles, slide under signs, collect snacks and earn one snack for every obstacle cleanly passed. Spend snacks between runs on spring soles, extra health, and a snack magnet. Runs and upgrades are saved in your browser.

Mobile: use the large on-screen buttons or tap the left/right halves of the game area. Desktop: Up/W/Space to jump, Down/S to slide, Escape to pause. Sound is optional.

Open index.html in a modern browser. All game resources are local; no server or external scripts required. The city backdrop was generated for this project; the character, gameplay objects, interface, and effects are drawn by code.
