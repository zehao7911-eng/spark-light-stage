LIQUID ATELIER
An original p5.js interactive pigment artwork, inspired by the supplied four interaction videos.
Open index.html, or serve this folder over HTTP. All assets are included; no network services required.
Touch / mouse: drag to stir, hold to pour, comb to separate ribbons, ripple to push concentric waves. Up to four simultaneous touch points. Mirror duplicates each gesture. Choose five palettes and five pigments; adjust brush size. Pause, fresh canvas, PNG export, optional synthesized sound and device tilt.
English UI. Portrait and landscape layouts. Mobile texture resolution bounded to 640 pixels on the longest edge and display DPR bounded to 1.5. WebGL1 required. Tilt availability depends on browser permissions and secure HTTPS. Export is handled by browser downloads.
This is a fluid-inspired GPU feedback artwork, not a scientific incompressible-fluid simulation. Palette and mirror preferences are saved locally. Canvas pigment is retained on viewport resize and device rotation; it is resampled to the new aspect ratio. No competitive scoring or artificial progression.
