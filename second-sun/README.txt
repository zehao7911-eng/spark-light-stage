SECOND SUN — The little repair atelier

An original, self-contained HTML5 repair puzzle game, inspired by the tactile repair direction of Assemble with Care. All artwork and synthesized audio are original. No reference-game assets are included.

Open index.html in a modern browser. No build system, CDN, external fonts or model downloads.

Five objects: radio, camera, clock, fan, music box.
Sweep dust with your finger/mouse. Hold screws to loosen them. Reconnect the circuit by turning tiles, align the lens rings, fit three gears, or repeat a four-note melody. Test the repair, then hold the power button to see the object come alive.
The Hint button can assist one connection or lens marker at a time. Completed objects and best star ratings save locally. The Shelf allows replay of unlocked objects. After all five repairs, new restoration batches vary circuit routes, lens angles and the music-box tune. A repair in progress restarts on reload; completed repairs stay saved.

Phone portrait / landscape and desktop are supported. Sound begins only after interaction. The simulation pauses when the tab is hidden.
