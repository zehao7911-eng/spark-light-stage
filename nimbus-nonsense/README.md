# Nimbus Nonsense

An original miniature weather-comedy game inspired by the playful cloud mischief of Rain on Your Parade (Steam 1213230). It includes eight neighborhoods, original procedural illustrations, rain-driven transformations, fleeing people, umbrella puzzles, thunder and balloon chain reactions, three hidden ducks per scene, splash chains, an album and earnable cloud outfits. No official artwork, code, music or levels are included.

Hold and drag on the scene to move your cloud and rain at the ground under your finger. Release to stop. Rain grows flowers, washes chalk into paper boats, soaks laundry and people, ruins cakes, fills fountains and reveals ducks. Thunder flips umbrellas for five seconds and pops nearby balloons; the gust can chain through adjacent balloons. It recharges in four seconds. Completed objectives keep their progress. After all main targets are transformed, collect your stars, or keep exploring for ducks first. Stars reward completion, all three ducks and a chain of at least three completed targets within 4.5 seconds of one another. Blush unlocks at six stars, Royal at fifteen. Eight original scenes; no endless unique-level claim.

Desktop: WASD or arrows move, hold Space to rain, T for thunder, Escape to pause. Touch and mouse share Pointer Events. The cloud sits above the touch point so its face stays visible. Separate horizontal and vertical layout placement keeps the full scene visible in portrait and landscape; illustrations retain their proportions. Large interface buttons, safe-area padding and bounded effects are included. Physical-phone testing is not claimed.

Open index.html directly, or upload all files to any static host. No install, network, account, CDN, WebGL, purchase or build step is required. A current browser with Canvas 2D and Pointer Events is needed. Audio starts after a gesture and can be muted. Backgrounding pauses and silences the game. Storage-denied browsers can still play; local saves, album and mute otherwise persist. Active rain is always released on reload, interruption or resize. The Postcard button downloads the scene as a PNG where supported.

p5.js 1.11.11 is bundled locally with P5-LICENSE.txt (LGPL 2.1). All remaining code, illustrations and synthesized audio are original. Rendering runs at a target of 45 FPS, simulation at 60 Hz, DPR at most 1.5. Puddles, effects and sound voices are bounded. No measured physical-device frame rate is claimed.
