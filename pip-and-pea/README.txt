PIP & PEA — A LITTLE STRETCH

An original, self-contained HTML5 gravity puzzle, inspired by Snakebird's fruit growth, whole-body support, body shapes and companion pushing. The artwork deliberately continues the dark pixel gardens, soft cream characters and sparse interface of the previous Pip Is You game, as requested. No extracted commercial artwork or generated images. All sprites, maps, code and synthesized audio are included locally. This is a compact independent adaptation, not the full commercial game.

PLAY
Open index.html directly, or keep all files together on a static HTTPS host. No installation, build step, external font, CDN or account is required.

Phone: swipe the board or hold a direction. Tap a companion or its portrait to switch.
Keyboard: arrows / WASD move. Q switches companions. Tab switches when the board has focus. Z undo. R restart prompt.

Each fruit adds one tail segment. A bird's complete shape falls if no segment rests on land, a resting companion, or a fruit. Fruit remains solid to falling bodies until eaten by an intentional head move. You can push a companion's whole shape when every destination cell is clear. Eat all fruit to wake the nest, then bring every bird's head to it. A bird returning home disappears and stops supporting companions, so arrival order can matter.

Thorns or falling beyond the board cause a tumble. Undo restores the exact earlier world, including removed fruit, bird length, positions and companions already at home. There is no life limit or timer. Undo stores the last 100 moves.

CONTENT
18 authored puzzles in three themed gardens. Every initial position is settled and every puzzle has a solver-verified winning route. Four completions in a chapter unlock the next chapter; all puzzles in an unlocked chapter may be selected. A daily puzzle changes each day. Concept hints and an optional restart-with-trail mode are included. The trail switches companions automatically while the player makes every directional move.

Saved in the browser: exact current world, active companion, undo history, active hint trail, chapter progress, best step counts, golden feathers and bird charms. Charms unlock at 4, 10 and 18 completed puzzles. Golden feathers require a finish without hints at or below the displayed reference route length. Audio is optional and starts after a user action. Browser storage may be cleared by the browser or platform; saves are local, not cloud accounts.

Mobile portrait and landscape layouts, pointer capture, touch repeat, accessible button labels, keyboard support and reduced-motion preference. Rendering slows while idle and skips hidden tabs. All art and sound work offline.

SCOPE
Fruit growth, gravity, body collision, old-tail-cell moves, pushing companions, independent selection, falling recovery and shared-nest exits are implemented. No teleporters, rotating level gravity or the full reference-game content.
