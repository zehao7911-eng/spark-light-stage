# Ember Cabinet
A tactile HTML5 fireplace inspired by Little Inferno's object experiments. Original illustrated toys and interface; no external assets or network dependencies.

Drag catalog toys into the hearth. Touch and hold empty space to ignite. Burning toys heat nearby objects. Each toy has a distinct reaction. Coins arrive automatically when an object finishes burning; discovered pairs grant bonuses. Six burns open Oddities, twenty open Wonder (combos unlock them sooner). Twelve pairs form rotating bonus orders; rewards increase with completed orders.

24 toys, three catalogs, 12 combinations, physical stacking, tossing, spreading fire, dynamic glow, synthetic sound, ash sweeping. Purchases always return more coins than they cost. There is no timer or loss condition. Progress saves locally. Unfinished toys are not restored after a reload; their purchase cost is refunded on recovery.

English interface. Pointer/touch and keyboard accessible catalog buttons. Responsive portrait and landscape. Pause and mute controls. No real-money features. Open index.html directly, or serve this folder. Editable source is included.
