A MOMENT OF STILLNESS

The grove can wait.

Drag the left stick or the ground to fly. WASD also works. Your ship fires at nearby enemies automatically; mouse aim is optional. Dodge the gold bullets. After clearing a grove, fly to the crystal at its center and rescue your friend.

Friends unlock dash (Space), twin shots, nova (Q) and a heart vessel. Follow the glowing gate to the next area. Return to Haven to spend crystals on upgrades. Progress saves automatically; retries keep earned crystals and enemy damage.
