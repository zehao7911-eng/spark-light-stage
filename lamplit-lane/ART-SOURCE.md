## Shared visual rules

The runtime lampkeeper, raised roof platforms, lanterns, UI frames, keepsakes, water strips and particle squares are original code-native pixel artwork in src/render.js and style.css. Character art uses a fixed 14-column clustered pixel pattern. Palette: navy #18283d, teal #365b66, jade #78a396, parchment #efe3bb, honey #efba61, copper #ba7861, lavender #9292b6. Lit roofs have warm top planes and darker copper sides; dark roofs stay blue. Gold is reserved for lanterns, hints, coins and completion. Distant town detail is dimmed behind interactive roofs. No commercial character, logo, complete UI or Steam art asset is reproduced. VT323 font is unmodified under FONT-OFL.txt. Sound is original synthesized audio without third-party samples.
