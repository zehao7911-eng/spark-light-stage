KOMOREBI LANE
An original HTML5 neighborhood game inspired by the cozy street-management direction of Minami Lane. All graphics are original code-drawn artwork; no assets from the reference game are included.

Open index.html in a modern browser. No build step, external libraries or network assets are required.
Tap shops matching waiting guests' illustrated wishes. Tips fund new shops, upgrades, gardens and a neighbor helper. Meet the day's happy-visit target, then welcome tomorrow. Pet cats, collect twelve memories and continue beyond the eight themed days.
Progress saves locally on this device. Sound starts only after a gesture. Phone portrait and landscape layouts are supported.
