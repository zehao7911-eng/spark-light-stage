PAPERBOUND — A JOURNEY BETWEEN THE FOLDS
Original HTML5 fold-map puzzle inspired by Paper Trail's foldable-world idea. Original canvas illustrations, traveler animation and synthesized audio; no commercial assets or external libraries.
Drag a paper edge inward, or tap its arrow, to flip its two-cell strip onto the map. Folding physically reflects cells, reverses their stacked layers and reveals their opposite face. Tap a folded arrow to unfold it. Undo reverses the most recent change during this session.
Peek behind shows the unfolded back as a planning aid. It does not change the active puzzle. Follow the path lights up when a route connects the traveler to the tent. The traveler walks through reachable paper flowers before arriving. Three flowers earn a complete chapter entry. Some routes can reach the tent before all three flowers are reachable; retry for the full entry.
Four original illustrated palettes: meadow, rain, amber and dusk. Seeded puzzles progress from one-fold routes to multiple-edge combinations, with different orientations and layouts. Every chapter is checked for a route collecting all three flowers. A gentle hint is available under Help. Chapters unlock in the book as you progress.
Current chapter, fold arrangement, move count, sound and best flower scores save locally. Undo history and walking animation do not persist across reloads. Hidden tabs pause animation. Completed pages can be downloaded as PNG postcards. English UI; mobile portrait, landscape and 44-pixel buttons. Standalone index.html, no build or network needed.
