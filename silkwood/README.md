# SILKWOOD — A tiny thread, a big world

An original HTML5 spider adventure inspired by Webbed's forest palette, layered pixel woodland and silk movement. Eight original groves plus generated Wild Trails. All artwork, levels, insignia and audio are original code-native work. This is a smaller browser adaptation, not the commercial game's complete story, maps or assets.

## Play
Open index.html directly or host this folder with any static server. No CDN, downloaded images, fonts, engine, login or network connection required. English interface. Tap a glowing branch point to attach a thread and reel in. Tap the same point or use Release to detach and keep momentum. Hold left/right to swing or move. Jump detaches with an upward lift when on a surface or attached.

Weave mode: tap two branches within range to build a persistent silk path. Walk and jump on it. Undo removes the last path; up to twelve paths per grove. The thread points and marked branches are attachable; the illustrated background tree trunks do not have full climbing collision.

Rescue three hanging silk moths, then reach the orchid at the far end. Optional pollen lights improve your medal. Follow a hint glimmer to the next moth or the exit. Falling below the grove returns to the latest reached branch; rescues, pollen and paths stay. Perch also returns there and counts as a return.

## Content and rewards
Eight groves: first threads, fern hollow, wind, bouncing mushrooms, rain scenery, night fireflies, canopy and orchid home. Wind affects movement; rain and night are visual themes. A three-star medal requires all three pollen and at most two returns. Records preserve best medals, time and pollen. Finishing a new grove earns three collection pollen, plus up to three collected lights; improved pollen records award only their difference. Collection pollen unlock Rose/Jade body colors. Replaying an already mastered grove does not repeatedly award currency.

After grove eight, Wild Trails continue with deterministically varied anchor heights, the eight environment styles and unlimited sequence numbers. Branch positions and core objectives vary within the same rescue structure; this is not the commercial game's full insect quest ecosystem.

## Controls
Touch/mouse: tap branches. On-screen left/right, Jump, Release, Weave, Undo, Hint and Perch. Desktop A/D or left/right arrows, Space or up jump, E release, W weave, Z undo, Escape pause. Pause contains help, collection, sound and restart. Large mobile controls and safe-area padding support portrait and landscape.

## Saves and offline
Current grove, body/rope state, paths, rescued moths, pollen, records, unlocks and cosmetics save locally, including on pause/visibility/page close. No offline simulation. Rendering caps at 30 FPS and reduces on menus; hidden tabs pause. Synthesized pluck/chime effects unlock through a gesture. Haptics are optional where supported. Browser local storage is needed for persistent saves.

Tested all eight original groves and sixteen Wild Trails in the rules engine; full eight-grove touch route, 24 rescues/24 pollen, persistent thread collision, undo, jump/release, checkpoint, pause, exact paused reload, medals, replay and cosmetic purchases. Responsive checks cover 390x844,360x640,844x390 and1280x800. Physical device speed varies.
