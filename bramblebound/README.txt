BRAMBLEBOUND — A little deck adventure

Open index.html in a modern browser, or serve this folder through any static HTTPS host. No downloads, remote fonts, libraries or accounts are required. English interface. Touch and keyboard-accessible controls. Automatic local save.

Play: choose a trail, select an enemy and tap cards. Spend three energy per turn. Block counters visible enemy attacks. Build a deck from rewards, upgrade at campfires, buy cards and relics, remove weak cards, and defeat all three guardians across 18 stops. A potion heals once per battle. Skip rewards to keep a focused deck. Three acts, 20 card designs, 11 relics, seven enemy designs, camps, shops, three encounters and three bosses.

Inspired by the visible-intent, deckbuilding and branching-route systems of Slay the Spire. This is a smaller original browser game; illustrations, characters, names, encounter content and audio are original. It does not contain the commercial game's assets or full content. No multiplayer or payments.

Save data stays in the browser. Begin journey replaces the previous run. A completed journey remains available for inspection until you start again. Sound starts only after interacting. Portrait hand scrolls horizontally; landscape supports smaller screens. HTML5 Canvas graphics scale to a maximum 2x pixel ratio.
