# A postal city in flat colour

The original shared visual grammar is architectural illustration: large hard-edged colour planes, no heavy black outline, limited warm stone and cool sky colours, restrained seams and deliberate repeated columns. Texture density comes from structured arches, ledges, shutters, paving and folded-paper facets rather than random noise.

Palette: cream `#fff1cf`, mint sky `#a9d7bd`, sandstone `#e6a74c`, sunlit edge `#ffdc8a`, terracotta shadow `#ba6b48`, berry accent `#a54451`, deep greenery `#346b5c`. Petal District adds muted rose and greenhouse glazing. Evening Embassy uses indigo shadows, warm pale windows, observatory silhouettes and a crescent moon.

Pom's ellipse is the playable silhouette. Folded side facets, eyes, blush and an envelope sit inside it. Limbs visibly tuck before contact; decoration is not a larger hidden collision box. The same geometry defines the aperture and fitting rules. The fitting projection remains faint so it cannot be mistaken for a second courier.

Composition: doorway above or to the right, waiting courier on a foreground platform, tapering bridge between them. Adjacent façades frame the scene, while the lower-contrast skyline supplies scale. A small canal, paper boat, flags, palms, plants and birds make the surroundings live without hiding the aperture.

UI: cream route label and slider below the scene, dark teal Send button, geometric travel stamps and compact serif headings. Header controls and all primary actions have at least 44 CSS-pixel targets. The path from shape → preview → send → fit/rebound → delivered stamp remains visible. No display of odds, cash shop or verbose editor tool panel.

Effects: local rebound, tucking pose, scale/depth travel, aperture clipping, a small ordered confetti burst and synthesized paper/chime tones. Decorative movement never changes the collision target. New districts extend this same grammar instead of mixing photorealistic, pixel and smooth vector assets.
