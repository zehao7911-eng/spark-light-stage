POCKET CAST
Original browser fishing hideaway inspired by WEBFISHING.

Hold CAST and release toward the pond. Wait for a bite. Hold REEL (Space) to reel in and tap YANK (E) to break gold barriers before the red escape meter catches up. On smaller screens, tap-to-reel defaults ON: tap REEL once, then tap YANK at barriers. Toggle it in the journal. Walk using the stick or WASD/arrows. Drag the scene to rotate the view.

Twelve illustrated fish species in three fishing spots. Discoveries, size records and catch counts save in the journal. Sell your basket for coins and buy rod power or a hat. Rod upgrades speed up reeling and break barriers with fewer taps. Unlock Tide Cove at five catches and Lantern Lake at twelve. Change lighting and jacket colours or download a postcard.

Touch portrait and landscape supported. Open index.html via an HTTP server. Three.js is included locally with its license. Original procedural models, illustrations and audio; no remote runtime dependencies. Local single-player experience; background anglers are decorative characters, not online players. This is an independent browser adaptation of the fishing loop, not the full commercial game or its assets. Progress is saved; an interrupted fishing attempt restarts safely after reload.
