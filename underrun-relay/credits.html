UNDERRUN · Pocket Relay

A mobile adaptation of UNDERRUN by Dominic Szablewski / Phoboslab. The original 3D renderer, pixel texture atlas, three PNG level maps, seeded world construction, physics and enemy/entity code are retained and modified under the MIT license. Original upstream commit: f933e29152d7fc1ca61d4b3eaa8b29551d7d7a62.

This is an adaptation of the complete source game, with its original authorship credited. It is not a new independently authored art set. The texture atlas adds one new navigation marker; the environments and character sprites are from the MIT project. See the author's making-of article.

What changed

Single-stick movement, automatic line-of-sight aiming and fire, ground-tap and radar waypoints, responsive pixel projection, route breadcrumbs, radar, EMP, dash, checkpoints, persistent killed threats and repaired relays, three between-sector suit upgrades, medals, save/redeploy/pause and mobile UI. Each original map is adapted to three active relay objectives, including a nearby introductory terminal. The original story terminal, Sonant-X synthesizer and music are excluded; the compact effect sounds in this version are newly synthesized.

Controls & rewards

Touch: drag the stick, hold its direction buttons, tap a floor tile, or set a waypoint on the radar. The suit automatically aims at visible threats and shoots. Approach a blue relay and stay nearby briefly to upload. Restoring a relay heals armor, charges EMP and saves a checkpoint. Dash grants a short dodge window. EMP damages nearby threats and clears their shots. Move to override a queued waypoint.

Keyboard: WASD / arrows move, Space dashes, E uses EMP, Escape pauses. Sound starts muted. Each sector awards stars for clearing its three relays, finishing within four minutes, and avoiding redeployment. High scores and best stars are saved locally. Reloading restores the current sector checkpoint/progress when browser storage is available. This browser game needs WebGL; graphics interruption displays a reload action.
