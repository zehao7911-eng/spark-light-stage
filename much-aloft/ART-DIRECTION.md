# MUCH ALOFT visual rules

Warm paper #f7edda, ink #263e43, ceramic teal #467c79, copper #d97743. Day skies are warm cream/sea-glass; middle skies lilac; night skies muted blue. All share warm wood, cream trim, small dark window recesses and a few brass highlights. Light UI surfaces and a teal primary control remain consistent; scene HUD changes to cream in night settings.

The aviator Shiba is a complete rounded, original geometric character, with ears, goggles, eye highlights, muzzle, nose, tongue, jacket/bib, articulated limbs, boots, curled tail, scarf, rotor and wings. Its visible gait, centred flip, compressed landing and stable glider pose carry the feedback. Outfit colour changes real jacket/arm materials. This is a meme-inspired character illustration, not an imported photograph.

Floating deck silhouettes are limited to warm plank circles, station platforms, cups and drums. Repeated screws, plank spacing, copper bands, recesses, narrow mullions, flower boxes and engine collars establish small-scale detail. Houses sit outside the playable route. Clouds use grouped smooth forms. Anchor lamps and rotors change when the player reaches milestones. The final airship responds to completion.

World feedback uses a soft foot contact decal, restrained spark pieces, golden rings, readable platform halos and small original number markers. The perspective camera frames the pilot and the next landing, with a safety snap if its tracked bounds leave the viewport. These bounds cover the required landing and pilot, not every distant decoration or later route step.

UI uses compact sans action labels and an italic serif for places/results; cream panels, thin green borders, rounded controls, letter-spaced small captions and gold star records. Menus remain native HTML controls with 44px minimum targets. Keep the same proportions and materials when extending the six route kits.

No downloaded art or image-generation output. Three.js is locally bundled; textures, maps and particles are created by the runtime code.
