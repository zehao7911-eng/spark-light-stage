MOTEWORKS
Small friends. Mighty things.

Open index.html in a current browser, or serve this folder as a static site.
The packaged index.html contains all scripts and styles. No CDN, account,
installation, model downloads or network calls are needed to play.

Tap the floor to walk. Drag to change your destination. Walk close to pink
friends to wake them. Tap a numbered object, or choose it in OBJECTS. Your
team automatically walks over, lifts it and takes it to the little cart.
The number is the free helpers needed. Helpers return after delivery.
Open the gold switch to release ribbons. WHISTLE recalls busy helpers and
puts down their deliveries. Interrupted deliveries cost the third medal.

Desktop: WASD / arrows to walk, Space to whistle, Escape to pause.
The circle button switches between follow and whole-desk views.

Six authored desks, nine golden motes per desk, up to eighteen medals,
four cosmetic coats. Finish all six to unlock Shuffle shifts: repeat the
six desks with alternating mirrored layouts. No timer or lives.
Medals: complete the deliveries / gather every mote / no delivery recalls.

Progress and the exact current shift are stored locally in this browser.
Closing, hiding or leaving the game pauses it. Continue shift resumes.
Restarting a desk replaces current positions, but preserves earned medals.
Save postcard downloads a PNG. Sound starts only after interaction.

Mobile: portrait and landscape touch controls, device pixel ratio capped
at 1.5, instanced helpers, batched scenery and 30 Hz drawing. WebGL loss
switches to a simple Canvas compatibility view with the same saved game.
Physical phone performance varies; mobile browser emulation is not a
substitute for testing your own device. ?flat=1 selects compatibility view.

Original browser-scale miniature carrying adventure inspired by Tinykin.
Geometry, art, interface and procedural sound are original for this project.
No commercial game assets or campaign are included.
