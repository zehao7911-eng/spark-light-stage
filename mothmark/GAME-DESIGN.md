# Game design / MOTHMARK

## Pillar

A small, readable specimen table. Two visitors, three tools, one tap per play. The cards remain the visual focus. There is no real-time deadline or gambling currency.

## Progression

Night 1 teaches Glow and Scent. Night 2 introduces Moon, the Lens and a wildcard jar. Night 3 adds the Keeper, who fills a sigil on both visitors. Night 5 adds a Seal, which finishes a visitor with at most two remaining sigils. New species arrive on nights 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10 and 12.

Each night has 12 plays plus two per Field Bag level. Visitor goals rise from three to six over time. The player can finish once the goal is met or continue for additional album duplicates and gold. The first two visitors prefer undiscovered available species; simultaneous visitors are distinct. If a hand has no legal match, one slot is repaired to an available required sigil. No player can be stranded by the tool draw.

## Economy and unlock effects

A specimen pays its species reward, plus 10 gold for its first discovery and a small rhythm bonus. Events add discovery gold, herb-use gold, an extra Moon point, or a third-capture reward. Night success adds a closing bonus and advances the next night. An incomplete night preserves progress, grants a small closing reward, and can be retried.

Lantern, Herbs and Lens gain one sigil of power every level. Extra matching power can reach the other visitor. Herbs level 2 adds a neighbour effect. Bag levels add plays; Bag level 2 adds one free sigil to each visitor at dusk. Four levels per track keep the growth readable. Purchases are restricted to between nights. The campaign concludes after successful Night 12 and supports endless continuation.

## Feedback

Completed sigils become checkmarks. The selected target has a gold bracket. Captures update the album and wallet immediately; a short discovery or reward message, palette-matched pixel bursts and a soft chord communicate the result. Unsupported tools are visibly disabled and also disabled in the accessible controls. Reduced motion removes idle phases, glints and bursts. Audio is optional.

## Art and performance

The refined 24-colour art family is retained. Twelve specimens and six tool tiles use the same native scale. The world is Canvas 2D with nearest-neighbour integer display scaling. The renderer is limited to approximately 30 updates per second, pauses while hidden and caps particle count. There are no downloaded engines or external asset services. The original art book is preserved separately from the game.
