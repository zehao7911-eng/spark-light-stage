Tidekeep

A compact three-island tower-defense game: place towers, mix fast shots with slowing and splash damage, upgrade between waves, and protect a little lighthouse. Three islands, six waves per island, a large final visitor, up to nine best stars.

Actual open-source foundation

Based on Tower Defense by Casmo / Mathieu, Copyright 2014, MIT licensed. Inspected source commit 784129a685627a18f3d3f8fa594208000d9313e8. The actual squared-distance range test, target-index validation and reacquisition, homing projectile calculation and enemy health/resource/score resolution are retained or adapted in source-combat.js. Unmodified Tower/Bullet/Enemy snapshots are included in upstream/ for comparison. The original project is a prototype; Tidekeep adds its own complete wave campaign, upgrades, saving, touch UI and new art.

Three.js r147 is bundled locally under its MIT license. No models, textures, music or interface graphics from the old prototype or a commercial game are reused. The round island, lighthouse, palms, ceramic turrets, animated sea creatures, particles and sounds were authored specifically for this adaptation using code.

How to play

Select Pebble, Frost or Shell, then tap an empty + pad. The first build starts the opening wave. Tap an existing tower to see its range, upgrade it, or sell it. Send later waves when your defense is ready. The Tide Bell pushes enemies back and damages them; stopping enemies recharges it. Space sends a wave or rings a charged bell. Escape pauses. Optional 2× speed shortens the wait.

Finish an island for a star, keep at least eight lighthouse hearts for a second, and finish without a leak for a third. Best stars and scores do not add twice on replay. Active waves, towers, enemies, projectiles and resources save locally when browser storage is available. Pausing or leaving the page stops sound and input. This game needs WebGL; interruption offers a reload action.
