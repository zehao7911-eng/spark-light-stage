# PRISM RIOT — Visual system

Reference family: Neon lines and luminous geometry, from the user-supplied atlas. Original subject: a faceted robotic conductor performing in three architectural light halls. Sharp silhouettes and narrow luminous seams apply equally to the character, notes, decks, towers, crowd and interface.

## Palette and signal grammar

Base ink #080B22, recessed surfaces #111A33 / #142A42, muted construction #365C71. Ion cyan #53ECED = triangle / left kick. Rose #FF6BBD = diamond / middle tone. Amber #FFCE70 = circle / right clap. Pale #E8FAFF is reserved for labels and count-in. Shapes identify inputs independently of colour. Unlockable rigs recolour this same three-signal system; they do not change gameplay or scale.

Edges: 1 to 1.5 CSS px for construction, 2 for interactive light, narrow glow radius 5 to 10px. Faces use two or three hard planes, not broad blurred blobs. A 54px character head anchors the proportions, pads share 32px glyphs, incoming note heads grow from 12 to 32px as they approach. Head and hands remain above the runway silhouette. Background architecture keeps only 10–25% signal brightness. No randomly distributed texture dots.

## Three spaces, one world

Ion Hall repeats chamfered portals and analogue meter towers. Glass Vault adds nested circular ribs. Solar Dome adds a radial roof. They share the same character scale, deck construction, tracks and dark floor. Receding lines, a foreground hit rail and prismatic audience establish depth in the 2D rendering. Wide framing reveals side galleries; portrait framing prioritises the head, notes and pads. Drawn details adapt to the viewport; this is not a cropped fixed screenshot.

## Motion and interface

Anticipation is the approaching note; contact is its shape meeting the rail; follow-through is a short outward shard burst, a pad depression and an upward score/chain response. Long notes have a visible tether and open ring. Misses use quiet grey feedback so the next note stays readable. Note geometry is never hidden by a screen-filling explosion. Combo grows audience motion, tower illumination and beams away from the hit rail. Calm Lights removes beam sweeps, particle motion and decorative bobbing. Paused scenery freezes.

Header icons, set numbers, thin borders, colour accents and the collection's repeated slots belong to the same light-console language. Menu text stays on opaque dark panels. Direct controls remain at least 44px. artbook.html renders a distant view, close character/detail samples, all three architectures, the shared palette and UI samples with the actual game drawing code. Its displayed charts are labelled art fixtures rather than player progress.
