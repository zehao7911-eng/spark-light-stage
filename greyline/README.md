# GREYLINE — Border Inspector

An original single-player HTML5 checkpoint inspection game, visually and mechanically informed by Papers, Please. It redraws its own pixel portraits, checkpoint, documents and insignia. It is not the original commercial game or its complete story/content.

## Start
Open index.html directly or serve the whole folder with any static web server. No external runtime, CDN, account, font, images or audio downloads. English UI. Responsive portrait/landscape touch and desktop controls.

## Five-shift campaign
Eight travelers per shift (40 total). Start with photograph and passport expiry; subsequent shifts add foreign entry permits, matching names, matching passport numbers, spoken purpose and genuine triangular seals. Norva citizens do not require permits. Today is printed above the desk. An expiry equal to today is valid.

Green admits; red denies. Watch the stamp strike, then read the decision receipt. Correct inspections earn salary, clean streaks add bonuses, and mistakes incur penalties. Heating and supper cost 10 credits per shift. Optional training notes add one check per shift; the brass stamp is a cosmetic upgrade. Earn accuracy medals and permanent discrepancy archive badges.

After the fifth shift, continue with generated endless watches or start a 90-second Rush. Rush measures active inspection time: menus and decision receipts pause the clock. Difficulty stays at all five rules. Campaigns generate new visitor data on restart; records persist.

## Inspect
Tap a document title to enlarge it. Compare mode: tap equivalent name/number fields on two papers, or compare the permit purpose with the traveler's speech. Tap the photograph, expiry or seal to check it directly. Two advisory checks are available per shift (three after training). Rules are always available. Desktop A/D approve/deny, R rules, Enter next after a receipt, Escape pause/back.

## Saves and performance
Campaign, current visitor, pending decision receipt, credits, upgrades, archive and best scores save locally. Closing the page or pausing saves. There is no offline time progress. Rendering caps at 30 FPS, reduces on modals and suspends on hidden tabs. Sound effects synthesize after a gesture and can be disabled. Browser storage is required for persistent records.

Verified portrait 390x844/360x640, landscape 844x390, desktop 1280x800, complete five-shift touch playthrough, document zoom, comparisons, receipts, upgrades, saved reload and Rush pause/end. Device performance can vary. Six local files: index.html, style.css, art.js, engine.js, game.js, README.md.
