# LITTLE LAP — Living Room Grand Prix

A complete, self-contained HTML5 toy-car racer reconstructed around the visible Table Top Turbo reference on Rezona. This edition uses new procedural models, a new circuit and new code; it is not the original game or a pixel-identical asset copy.

## Play
Open standalone.html directly, or serve this folder and open index.html. Requires a WebGL browser. English UI. No CDN, account, tracking, payment or external assets.

Keyboard: WASD / arrows drive, Space holds drift, release for charged turbo, R recovers, Esc pauses. Touch: use left/right, gas, brake and drift buttons simultaneously, or hold the scene to accelerate and steer toward either screen edge. Steering assist is on by default; disable it in the garage for free steering with braking before tight corners.

## Included
One detailed living-room circuit with an elevated cardboard section crossing the coffee table, three laps, four racers, racing positions, time trial, lap timing, boost strips, drift sparks and tyre trails, original synthesized engine and effect sounds (muted by default), minimap, pause, recovery, quality settings, best time and persistent podium rewards. Three starting paints; Lilac unlocks at 1 podium and Midnight at 3. No online multiplayer, additional tracks, full rigid-body physics or accurate reproduction of the reference's unique car models. In assisted mode cars are constrained to the track. Manual mode has free planar steering and off-road recovery; decorative furniture has no general-purpose rigid-body collision solver.

## Mobile
Responsive portrait and landscape controls, minimum 44px active touch controls, render cap 30fps and no live sun shadow in automatic small-screen quality. Backgrounding pauses and releases controls. Actual physical-phone GPU performance varies and is not certified. Race state itself is not resumed across reload; personal best, colors, podiums and settings are.
