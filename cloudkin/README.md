# Cloudkin Isles

A small original pixel-art creature-collecting adventure inspired by the island exploration and spirit encounters of Moonstone Island, and by the pixel-art categories in My Game Art Style. It is not a recreation of the original game's full content or assets.

Tap the island to move, tap a spirit to meet it, and choose Bop, Offer, or Guard. Lower a spirit's energy to make your offers stronger, then reach six bond to befriend it. Find six spirits across three unlockable islands. Crystals earn stardust; rest restores health. Your progress is saved locally in your browser. Travel revisits islands and restores their encounters and crystals.

Mouse/touch: tap to walk and use menus. Keyboard: WASD or arrow keys to move; use mouse for encounters and menus. Open index.html in a modern browser. All game assets are local. Pixel scenery was generated for this game; characters, effects, and interface were created for the game.
