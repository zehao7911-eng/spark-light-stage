PULSE PALS
An original single-button rhythm clinic.

Tap or press Space on the seventh beat. Six original tracks introduce quiet beats, swing, tempo changes and double hearts. Earn stars to meet the next friend. Best results and audio preferences save locally.

All assets and synthesized music are included. No external dependencies. Serve this folder over HTTP; index.html is the entry point. Supports portrait and landscape touch screens. Music starts after Start. Timing offset is available in Friends.

Inspired by the seven-beat concept in Rhythm Doctor; no commercial assets or music are included.
