BOUNCE MAIL — original HTML5 action game

Drag anywhere in the play field to move the spring. Catch falling packages.
The contact point steers the bounce: the left half sends left; right half sends
right. Triangle mail goes to the coral carriage, circle mail to the blue carriage.
A center hit gently sends mail toward its matching side. Watch the dotted guide.
Keyboard: left/right arrows, Space to pause.

Finish a delivery quota, collect a landscape stamp, choose a tool upgrade, and
start the next route. Four changing landscapes, increasing concurrency/speed,
gold bonus packages, chain rewards and endless routes. Four mistakes end a run.
Wider spring, extra heart and longer flight guide are permanent tools and survive new runs.
Best score and collected stamps survive new runs. Automatic local save with
Continue route on return. Hidden tabs pause. Route book shows earned postcards.

All code/art/audio original. p5.js1.11.11 bundled LGPL2.1 (P5-LICENSE.txt).
No CDN, external artwork, image generation or video; Canvas2D procedural art.
Open index.html directly or publish this whole folder on a static host.
English UI, phone touch and desktop keyboard/mouse, portrait and landscape.
localStorage may be unavailable in private/blocked storage; game still runs.
Original small action game; not a copy of any commercial Steam game.
