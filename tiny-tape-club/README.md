# TINY TAPE CLUB
An original compact musical toy playground inspired by Steam ODDADA (https://store.steampowered.com/app/1627870/ODDADA/). Original code, geometry, UI, synthesis and compositions; no commercial game artwork, models, audio or maps are included. This is an independent small interpretation, not the complete commercial game.

## Play
Tap any toy or the eight step buttons to cycle rest / low / middle / high. Each layer uses its own instrument. Start/stop the loop, change tempo, mute individual layers, adjust their volume and undo up to 40 edits. Right click a step to cycle backwards. Keyboard 1-8 edits, Space plays/pauses, Z undoes, Escape opens the menu. F switches finale after its introduction.

Twelve short creative ideas guide you from a heartbeat to a four-layer loop. A satisfied idea must remain valid for at least eight playing steps and reach the loop boundary before its stamp can be claimed. Bells unlock at two stamps, bass at five, choir at eight. Four stamps unlock Dusk colour, eight unlock Candy. Finale adds extra percussion and stronger bounces. There is no timer, combat, gambling or failure screen. After all twelve ideas, free music-making continues; these are twelve finite goals, not infinitely distinct levels.

Save up to twelve cassettes, load or delete them with confirmation, export four repeats of a mix to stereo 44.1kHz 16-bit WAV, or save an island PNG. WAV contains your actual notes, tempo, layer volumes/mutes and finale percussion, plus a short release tail. Global speaker mute does not silence file exports. Browsers may present downloads differently on phones. First use requires a tap to allow Web Audio.

Progress, patterns, volume, unlocks, undo and cassettes save to this browser's localStorage. Reload starts paused at the beginning of the loop. Clearing browser data removes saves. No cloud/account synchronization. Saved tapes are local data, not automatic external sharing. Clearing a layer and loading a tape can be undone; claiming an idea clears undo history while retaining musical notes. Save slots retain current settings, but playback always starts from a loop boundary after a reload.

## Art and technology
Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility renderer, original batched smooth 3D toy meshes, baked directional vertex shading, two-column phone sculpture layout, wooden island layers, eight individually spring-animated toys, conductor and track train. Render capped at 40FPS. Twelve-stamp celebration particles capped at 36. DOM overlay and Web Audio provide browser interaction, sample-accurate look-ahead note scheduling and offline WAV rendering. Godot owns patterns, objectives, saves and 3D animation. The editor alone does not play the Web Audio score; use the Web export for full sound/export functionality. No CDN, remote libraries, samples, fonts, images or required cross-origin-isolation headers. WebGL2 and WebAssembly required; approximately 40MB initial engine download. Responsive browser-size tests do not certify every physical phone.

Serve the web folder over HTTP(S), e.g. python -m http.server 8000. Double-clicking index.html is unsupported by Godot. Source is in godot-source/ in the ZIP. Open project.godot with Godot 4.7; export Web, then insert ui.html immediately before the exported closing body and copy ui.js/ui.css/audio.js beside index.html. Single-thread Web export preset is included. The ordinary website does not contain development test fixtures.
