Palmline GT

An original browser arcade racer. Three routes, traffic, slipstream, turbo, checkpoint timers, medals and a persistent paint garage. All interface text is English. Keyboard and touch controls are supported.

Actual source adaptation

Uses the MIT-licensed Javascript Racer by Jake Gordon. The projection, utility functions and polygonal road renderer in racer-core.js are retained from common.js. Road construction and the perspective rendering loop are adapted from v4.final.html. See the complete original MIT notice.

The upstream example's borrowed OutRun sprites and separately licensed music are not included. Cars, roadside props, backgrounds, UI, synthesized sound and all progression rules were newly created for this project.

Design reference: the accessible road-racing and scenic arcade presentation of Slipstream. No assets, branding or proprietary code from Slipstream are used. This is an independent game.

Controls

Automatic acceleration. Left/right arrows or A/D steer. Space activates turbo. Down/S brakes. On touchscreens, hold the large steering and pedal buttons. Following traffic replenishes turbo; passing safely builds flow. Checkpoints restore time. Improve your best route score to earn credits; paints cost 350 credits. Three routes are available from the beginning.

Sound starts muted. Tap the music button to enable synthesized engine and reward sounds. Backgrounding pauses a race. Progress is saved locally when browser storage is available.
