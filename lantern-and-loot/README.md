# Lantern & Loot

A compact HTML5 exploration and shopkeeping adventure inspired by the dungeon-to-shop loop of Moonlighter. Original pixel artwork, local Canvas renderer, no external libraries or assets.

## Play

Explore one of three ruin regions. Move with the touch stick, tap a destination, or use WASD/arrows. Swing with the sword button/Space; dodge with the dash button/Shift. Break pottery, defeat telegraphed enemies, open nearby chests with Interact/E, and collect finds by walking near them. Clear a room and follow the north gate to go deeper. Four rooms end in a keeper encounter. Return/R keeps the haul; defeat returns you with half the current bag and all permanent upgrades intact.

Back home, choose shelf items and set Deal, Fair or Bold prices. Customers walk in, inspect shelves, react to prices and buy from the same inventory you brought home. Open shop runs a short eight-customer session. Sales fund sword damage, backpack capacity, health and display upgrades. Blade levels unlock two harder regions. A first special order rewards selling three Sun shards. Twelve goods have different values; the keeper carries a valuable Gate sigil.

Progress and active expeditions save locally when browser storage is available. Hidden pages and modal menus pause simulation. Audio starts after interaction; optional vibration. English UI, mobile portrait/landscape layouts, keyboard and pointer support. Serve this folder on any static host or open index.html directly.

This is a focused single-player game, not the complete content or mechanics of its reference. There are three regions, four rooms per expedition, three enemy behaviors and a shared inventory/economy loop.
