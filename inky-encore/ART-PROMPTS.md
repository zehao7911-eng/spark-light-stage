## Actor and host atlas

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent sprite atlas for the existing original HTML5 puzzle INKY ENCORE. Create ONE precisely arranged 4-column by 4-row sprite sheet, a square image ideally 2048x2048, sixteen equal cells, actual transparent alpha throughout the background. No labels, no text, no borders, no grid, no scenery. Every subject centered within its cell, full body visible, generous 12 percent empty padding on every cell edge, no touching or overlapping neighbouring cells. All sprites share a polished original 1930s rubber-hose cartoon illustration style: wonderfully expressive pie-cut eyes, crisp varied dark-plum ink outlines, cream white gloves with correct fingers, curving black hose arms and legs, oversized glossy oxford shoes, restrained warm cel shadows, subtle hand-painted colour texture, charming appealing poses. Sophisticated animation production design, not crude geometric clipart. Colours are coral red, muted jade blue, warm brass gold, parchment ivory, and deep plum. Consistent front three-quarter view and line weight across all sixteen.
