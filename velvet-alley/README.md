# Velvet Alley

An original, compact 3D HTML5 cat exploration game inspired by Little Kitty, Big City's curious black cat, warm Japanese streets, vertical exploration, animal friends and delightful hats. The city, character geometry, animations, interactions and synthesized audio are original; no commercial game assets are included. This is a smaller browser adaptation, not the full commercial game or its map.

Follow six fish through nineteen connected perches. Fish strengthen your jumps; all six unlock the final window. Tap the gold perch or JUMP to take a generous animated leap. Use the joystick, WASD, or click the open street to walk. E / PAW changes to the nearby interaction. Discover three gravity-loving flower pots, play a cat-sized football game, collect nineteen shinies, befriend the crow and ginger cat, take a garden catnap, and finally return home. Three hats unlock through discoveries and stay available on another day. The notebook lets you choose a discovery and its next-hop trail. The street button returns you to a safe starting point without losing discoveries.

English interface; touch portrait and landscape layouts; local progress and costumes. Interrupting a jump restores its last safe perch. Production pauses when hidden and the pause menu stops simulation. Another day restarts discoveries but keeps your hats and accumulated shinies. No sign-in, ads, network, CDN, external font or image generation. The packaged index.html is entirely self-contained and works offline; serve it with any static host or GitHub Pages. Requires WebGL. Three.js r147 is bundled under the accompanying MIT license.
