# NEVER AFTER — The Wilderness

An original, hand-inked single-player HTML5 wilderness survival game. Its visual direction and survival structure reference Don't Starve Together; it is not the commercial game's artwork, multiplayer implementation or complete content.

## Play
Open index.html, or host this folder on any static web server. Everything is included locally: no CDN, external images, fonts, account or installation. Touch a resource to walk over and gather it. Use the joystick or tap the ground to move. Craft an axe, collect logs and grass, then light the nearby camp before nightfall. Eat berries or cook meals near a lit fire. Fuel a camp with logs.

Desktop: WASD/arrows move, E use, Space strike, F eat, C craft, Escape pause.

## Survival loop
Harvest trees, grass, berries, carrots and rocks. Craft axes, pickaxes, spears, torches, campfires, meals, gardens, tents and lanterns. Watch health, hunger and sanity; darkness and winter cold are dangerous. Stay near a fire or lantern at night. Fight approaching spiders, discover journal badges, survive seven nights and continue indefinitely. Gardens produce food and tents restore health at dawn. Autumn, winter and spring cycle every four days.

The map and camp shortcut help navigation. Crafting and menus pause time. Runs, inventory, buildings, discoveries and best survival records save locally. There is no offline time progression. Gentler mode is available after a failed run. The sound toggle controls synthesized interaction effects.

## Compatibility
Canvas 2D rendering; responsive portrait and landscape touch controls; capped display density and reduced idle frame rate. Local saves require browser storage. No WebGL or 3D assets. Mobile viewport tests cover 360x640, 390x844 and 844x390. Device performance varies.

## Files
index.html, style.css, art.js, engine.js, game.js and this README. Original procedural artwork and level layout. No original game's assets are distributed.
