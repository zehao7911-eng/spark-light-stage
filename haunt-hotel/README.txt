HAUNT HOTEL — original HTML5 action / light progression game

Tap or drag to walk; the worker finds a path around solid furniture. Approach
ghosts to vacuum automatically. Clean nearby dust. Finish both counters.
Blast (Space) breaks bubble shields and briefly stuns nearby ghosts. Cooldown7s.
Keep furniture safe and keep your hearts to earn more stars. A room takes up to
100seconds. Failure lets you retry the same room with all equipment and coins.
Keyboard arrows / WASD to move, Space blast, P pause.

Three permanent upgrade tracks, each three levels: hose range, movement speed,
extra hearts. Earn coins at room completion. Replayed rooms give smaller rewards.
Four hotel palettes, regular/swift/shield ghosts and a crowned boss every fourth
floor, increasing ghost counts and furniture layouts, six introductory rooms,
then endless nights with tougher ghosts and remixed spawns. Room book replays unlocked floors (changing an active room
requires a second confirmation tap). Automatic local save with Continue shift.
Best floors, stars, coins and tools persist. Hidden tabs pause. Optional synthesized
sound. Mobile touch, portrait/landscape and desktop controls.

All art, gameplay and sound original, code-drawn Canvas2D; no image generation,
commercial assets, WebGL or CDN. p5.js1.11.11 bundled under LGPL2.1; see license.
Open index.html offline or put this whole folder on a static host.
Private/blocked localStorage can disable save; ordinary play still works.
