# Window Wonder

An original compact HTML5 toy boutique. Directionally inspired by the display/pricing/customer loop of Toy Shop Simulator (Steam 3163790), with its own illustrated miniature, toys, economy, shipments and synthesized audio. No commercial artwork, code, music, characters or image generation is used. This is not a port or a full shop simulator.

## Play

Swipe the parcel's golden tape, or tap Peel tape five times. Pop the twelve bubble-wrap bubbles (optional). Drag each toy from the arrivals tray onto any shelf. You can also tap a toy, then tap a shelf. Dropping onto another toy swaps the pair. Tap a displayed toy, then PRICE to cycle $4 / $7 / $10.

Put two toys from the same family next to each other, horizontally or vertically. Both receive a $3 set bonus at checkout. Families are sky, garden, sea and home; the toys are visibly related by subject. There are two toys per family in every shipment, so three complete pairs are always possible. No forced matching outlines or customer hand-deliveries.

Open shop when all six toys are displayed. Animal shoppers browse, admire, collect and pay. Budget cycles through $4/$7/$10; the price applies before the bonus. Expensive goods may be passed over until a richer visitor arrives. There is no failure clock and no wasted stock. Tap an admiring or paying shopper to say hello: a heart appears, they move a little faster, and leave a $1 tip (once per purchase). The ×2 button speeds up the simulation. Wait for every payment to finish before the delivery reward is deposited. A sale adds its toy to your collection.

Earn one star for clearing a delivery, one for selling all three pairs, and one for popping all bubbles. Eight named shipments introduce all twelve illustrated toys. Further shipments cycle those eight manifests with deterministic within-family variants; they are not unlimited hand-authored levels. The journal replays unlocked deliveries after confirmation. Twenty-four best stars, all twelve toys, coin savings and Sage/Rose/Porcelain shelf finishes persist. Rose costs $40 and Porcelain $80, each paid once. Sales fund these decorations; there is no real money, advertising or paid random reward. A fresh replay can earn normal delivery revenue.

Keyboard: arrows select a shelf; Space selects/places a toy or peels tape; Tab reaches buttons; Enter/Space activates a button; Escape/P pauses. Use tap selection if dragging is difficult. Pausing, blurring or hiding freezes the illustration and model, releases touches and silences audio. Current parcel cuts, bubbles, stock arrangement, prices, customer movement and unsettled revenue save, restoring with input released. Storage denial is tolerated for the current session. Malformed saves fall back to a fresh game. Clearing browser data erases progress. Save postcard exports the actual current canvas as PNG.

Open index.html directly from the extracted ZIP, or serve this directory on any static host such as GitHub Pages. All six files are local. No build, server API, WebGL, account, CDN, external font or download is required. Canvas2D, 60Hz fixed logic, 40FPS render target, DPR capped at 1.5, at most four customers, 120 particles and fourteen audio voices. Portrait and landscape have separate compositions and respect safe-area insets. Browser touch emulation is not physical phone certification.
