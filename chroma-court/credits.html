Chroma Court

A one-finger fluid paddle game. Return the light, play the angles and earn your place on four luminous courts. Match scores, best medals, trails and an unfinished match are stored on this browser when storage is available.

Actual open-source foundation

This game uses the numerical fluid solver and adapts the paddle/ball logic from Fluid Table Tennis by Anirudh Joshi. The upstream solver credits Oliver Hunt (2009), Anirudh Joshi (2012) and Memo Akten (2008–2009), and implements real-time fluid techniques described by Jos Stam.

fluid-core.js retains the original density advection, velocity projection, boundary conditions and RGB solver. The obsolete DOM renderer/polyfill were removed, source forces are cleared per update for stability, interpolation temporaries are locally scoped and field arrays are exposed to avoid per-pixel allocation. rules.js adapts the original angular paddle bounce, wall reflection and goal/reset rules into a portrait simulation, with swept contacts, movement spin, AI, bounded fluid influence, scoring and progression.

The complete original MIT notices are included in LICENSE-FLUID.txt and in the retained solver. Original source snapshots are included under upstream/. See README.md for exact provenance, changes and how to run the included tests. This is an independent adaptation, without upstream advertising, external fonts, images, music or network services.

All glass-court frames, lighting, botanical medallions, paddles, particles, interface and synthesized sound were created for Chroma Court. No commercial-game artwork is used.

Play

Slide anywhere on the court to move your paddle. Portrait uses left/right motion; landscape rotates the court and uses up/down motion. Arrow keys and A/D also work; Up/Down are natural for landscape. Edge hits angle the return. Moving quickly through contact adds spin and a stronger shot. Four returns charge FLOW SURGE; tap its button or Space to temporarily widen the paddle and strengthen returns.

Neon Reef and Amber Arcade play to three, Velvet Garden to four, and Aurora Hall to five. A shutout win earns three stars; conceding one earns two; any other win earns one. Each court retains its best medal, rather than adding rewards again. Trail colors unlock at 3, 6 and 9 total stars. Win a court to unlock the next. Rematches vary the seeded serve/AI pattern.

Sound starts muted. Pause or background the page to freeze play and suspend audio. A saved match restores to a pause screen so you can prepare before resuming. The ball, paddles, score, RNG and charge are restored; transient fluid textures are refreshed rather than stored as a large image. No login, purchases or analytics are required.
