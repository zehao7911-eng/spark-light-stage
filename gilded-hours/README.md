# GILDED HOURS

Six original stained-glass clock puzzles, inspired by Glass Masquerade's Art Deco exhibition framing, irregular glass fitting and quiet restoration feedback. All illustrations, fragment geometry and interface code are original; no commercial assets or original puzzles are included.

Moonwing, The Jade Current, A Thousand Feathers, The Astral Voyager, The Ember Fox and The Orchid Hour contain 18, 24, 30, 36, 42 and 48 fragments. There are 198 pieces across the collection. Piece cuts form deterministic Voronoi partitions of a circular clock; each cut and image belongs to exactly one position.

## Play
Select a coloured fragment and tap its place, or drag it onto the clock. Correct placements snap gently and play a glass chime. Wrong attempts keep the fragment selected; no lives, countdown or penalties. Gold dots identify outer-edge pieces. Page the tray to see more fragments. Undo removes your most recent placement. Glimpse shows the finished art for three seconds; Hint outlines the selected piece's position for six seconds. After restoring a clock, its hands begin moving and it remains in your collection. All six clocks are available from the gallery, and unfinished progress is saved individually.

Restoring one clock unlocks Night Exhibition in the gallery. Night pieces initially appear as silhouettes and show their artwork when selected or dragged. Day and Night progress are separate. Replay restarts the active puzzle without erasing collection medals or the other mode's progress. Keyboard: Tab/Enter select controls and pieces, H hint, P glimpse, Backspace undo, Escape deselect.

## Offline and mobile
The packaged index.html is self-contained, with local synthesized audio and procedural Canvas2D art. No library, font, server, CDN or WebGL is required. Open index.html after unzipping. Separate source files are also included for editing.

The interface uses English and supports portrait and landscape. Dragged pieces lift above the finger; tap-to-place avoids finger occlusion. Browser tests cover real touch events in mobile emulation at 390x844, 360x640 and 844x390. These are emulated viewports, not measurements on physical iPhone/Android hardware. Pixel ratio is capped at 2; art, fragment thumbnails, background and placed-piece layers are cached. Rendering pauses when the tab is hidden.

Original source and levels may be edited freely by the project owner. Inspiration reference: https://store.steampowered.com/app/511470/Glass_Masquerade/ . This is an independent browser interpretation, not the original commercial game.
