# Art source and unified direction

Original artwork was made with the built-in image generation tool. WebP files are lossless format conversions; no outside commercial game assets were copied.

- [stage.webp](stage.webp): 1672 × 941, ruined observatory combat platform.
- [sprites.webp](sprites.webp): 1448 × 1086 RGBA, 4 × 3 character atlas. A second generation corrected gutters and kept weapons within cells.
- [cards.webp](cards.webp): 1536 × 1024, 6 × 5 card-illustration atlas. 27 game card definitions select art from this atlas; unused tiles are retained as source.
- Symbols and fallback card pictures: code-native integer-coordinate Canvas drawings in `src/app.js`.
- Palette: ink navy `#121d2a`, weathered jade `#335c60`, mint `#a5c7ae`, warm ochre `#b88645`, ivory `#ead9ad`, ember coral `#d56b55`.
- Scene hierarchy: low-contrast distant stone, bright interactable characters, pale parchment cards, brass borders and cost gems. Card illustration crops and effects use pixelated rendering. Floating numbers, small square fragments and limited screen shake preserve target readability. Reduced-motion preference is respected.
