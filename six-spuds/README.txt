SIX SPUDS
Original HTML5 arena roguelite inspired by Brotato.

Move with the touch stick, drag anywhere on the arena, or use WASD / arrows. Weapons aim and fire automatically. Survive twenty waves, gather green materials and choose upgrades between waves. Six weapon slots. Buying the same type and tier combines it into the next tier (up to IV). Buy items, lock offers before rerolling or recycle weapons in your inventory. At least one weapon must remain. The final wave ends only when the Beetle King is defeated.

Four starting characters. Six weapons: pistol, SMG, shotgun, spear, ember wand and circular saw. Eight shop items and six level-up attributes. Three ranged weapons grant damage; three melee weapons grant armor; two elemental weapons strengthen burn damage. Cozy and Standard difficulty options.

Pause or tab away safely. Shop state, purchases, upgrades and run records save locally. Reloading during a battle returns to that wave checkpoint, restoring its starting currency, experience and health. A wave cannot be farmed through reloads.

Serve this folder with HTTP. index.html is the entry point. No external assets or libraries required. Original illustration and synthesized sounds. Supports touch portrait / landscape and keyboard. This is a standalone single-player adaptation of the arena / build loop, not the complete commercial game, its original artwork, its hundreds of items or its co-op modes.
